Game modes

How a story in the Four Nations leans.

Between the Worlds

SPECIALIST · Medium
The party are touched by the Spirit World, seeing and speaking with what others cannot.
Expect · spirits, visions, old debts
Lighter on · plain battles

Blood and Moon

SPECIALIST · Very hard
A waterbender who has felt the water in living things, and must decide what that makes them.
Expect · full moons, secrets, terrible power
Lighter on · clean victories

Chaos

CHAOS · Very hard
At a great event the Avatar cycle shatters, and every player character wakes as an Avatar.
Expect · many Avatars, a frightened world, a hunt for all of you
Lighter on · the canon's order

Comet Rising

POWER · Easy
The party are prodigies, strong enough to change battles, with the world taking notice.
Expect · big bending, quick growth, heroic wins
Lighter on · slow training

Dai Li Agent

ROLE · Hard
The party serve the Dai Li inside Ba Sing Se's walls, keeping the city calm and the war a secret.
Expect · intrigue, secrets, moral pressure
Lighter on · open battle

Fire Nation Deserter

ROLE · Hard
The party have walked away from the Fire Nation's war and are hunted by their own people.
Expect · pursuit, disguise, divided loyalty
Lighter on · rest

Freedom Fighter

ROLE · Hard
The party are a band of fighters against the occupation, living in the trees and striking from cover.
Expect · raids, ambushes, hard choices
Lighter on · comfort and safety

Kyoshi Warrior Band

ROLE · Medium
The party are Kyoshi Warriors, the island's sworn protectors, sent out into the war.
Expect · discipline, teamwork, honour
Lighter on · solo heroics

Lightning's Edge

SPECIALIST · Hard
A firebender who can call lightning, the royal art, and must live with what it takes.
Expect · precision, calm under pressure, deadly power
Lighter on · rage

Metal and Stone

SPECIALIST · Medium
An earthbender who can feel the earth inside metal, in a world that says it is impossible.
Expect · discovery, armour broken, wide eyes
Lighter on · tradition

Multiplayer

TABLE · Medium
A party of benders and nonbenders from different nations, travelling one road through the war.
Expect · shared scenes, a spotlight for everyone, mixed nations
Lighter on · solo arcs

Pai Sho Initiate

ROLE · Medium
The party have been noticed by an old order of Pai Sho players who quietly move the world's tiles.
Expect · secrets, mentors, quiet influence
Lighter on · open war

Roots of the Swamp

SPECIALIST · Medium
A waterbender of the swamp's way, bending water through vines, roots and fog.
Expect · visions, strange allies, the one great tree
Lighter on · cities

Sandbender Clan

SPECIALIST · Medium
The party belong to a sandbender clan of the Si Wong, living by the desert's law.
Expect · dunes, water law, clan feuds
Lighter on · cities

Southern Raider's Conscience

ROLE · Hard
The party serve on a Southern Raiders ship, or once did, and are starting to see what it has done.
Expect · guilt, discipline, revenge on all sides
Lighter on · easy redemption

Tea at the Jasmine Dragon

GENTLE · Very easy
A slower, kinder road: tea, friendship and small troubles, with the war far away.
Expect · warm scenes, kind strangers, small stakes
Lighter on · battles and loss

The Long Road

ROLE · Medium
The party are refugees on the war's roads, looking for safety, papers and each other.

Lighter on · fights

The Other Avatar

The player is the Avatar of this cycle, in Aang's place, with the whole world's hope and hunt on them.

The Third Eye

SPECIALIST · Hard
A rare firebender born with combustion: fire from the mind, released as a blast.
Expect · explosions, assassins, being feared
Lighter on · subtlety
