Smoked Elephant Koi

Thick slabs of the giant bright-scaled koi that is Kyoshi Island's chief export, smoked over the island's fires. Rich and oily, one slab is a full meal and keeps a traveller fed and warm through a cold day at sea. 4 Pieces, sold at Kyoshi Island.
