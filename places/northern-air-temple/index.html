Northern Air Temple

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 9-24 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

One of the four Air Temples, on a peak of the Taihua Mountains along the continent's north coast, emptied in the Air Nomad Genocide and settled a generation ago by Earth Kingdom refugees under the mechanist after a flood took their village. His machines and his son Teo's gliders have filled the temple with steam, pipes and noise. A traveller sees first the gliders in the wind, then the smoke where no smoke should be.

Areas

  • Glider Hall: The hall where Teo and the children fly their gliders from, the wind roaring through the open side of the mountain.
  • Mountain Road Station: The station at the foot of the climb where the mountain coaches turn and the yard clerk sells passage; above it the lift to the temple.
  • The Old Sanctuary: The airbenders' sanctuary, its statues and murals half hidden behind steam pipes.
  • The Workshop: The mechanist's workshop, inventions and half-inventions everywhere, and a locked door nobody discusses.
