Gan Jin Camp

[Recommended Level: 9-18 · Danger: Moderate]

The camp of the Gan Jin tribe on the Great Divide's rim, refugees bound for Ba Sing Se, tidy, clean and proud of it, and feuding with the Zhang over a hundred-year-old wrong. A traveller is offered clean water and a lecture.
