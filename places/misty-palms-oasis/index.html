Misty Palms Oasis

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 9-27 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A town on the south-western edge of the Si Wong Desert, grown from a seedy cantina around a spring of ice that is melting. Traders, soldiers, sandbenders and wanderers crowd it, because it is the last place to rest and buy before the desert and the first place to drink after it. A traveller sees first the gate, the palms, and the cantina's doorway where everyone who crosses the Si Wong is sooner or later seen.

Areas

  • Caravan Gate: The gate where the caravans and sand-sailers assemble, the yard clerk's booth beside it and the fares to the four roads chalked up.
  • The Cantina: The oasis cantina: mango drinks, sandbenders at the back, a professor with maps, and every rumour of the desert for the price of a drink.
  • The Spring: The oasis's ice spring under the palms, shrinking every year, where the town's water is drawn and its quarrels begin.
