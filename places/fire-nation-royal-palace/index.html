Fire Nation Royal Palace

[Recommended Level: 16-30 · Danger: Severe]

The home of the Fire Nation Royal Family and the seat of its government, at the centre of the crater city, guarded day and night. Behind its gates are the throne room where Fire Lord Ozai sits behind a wall of flame, the royal apartments, the war room where the generals plan the comet, and the gardens where Zuko and Azula were children. A traveller sees it only from the gate unless summoned, and a summons is rarely good news.

Areas

  • Fire Throne: The long hall to the Fire Lord's throne, the flames before it rising with his temper and the courtiers kneeling at a distance measured in fear.
  • Royal Gardens: The pond and the turtle ducks, where a mother once taught her son to be kind.
  • War Room: The map room where the generals plan the war and the Fire Lord decides what to burn next.
