Great Gates of Azulon

[Recommended Level: 14-30 · Danger: Severe]

The sea gate of the Fire Nation Capital, built early in Fire Lord Azulon's reign: a great net held between statues of Azulon and two dragons, raised across the strait and set alight when a ship comes that should not. A traveller passes under it on any ship that enters the harbour, and the crew goes quiet.

Areas

  • The Strait: The water between the statues, the net sunk below the surface and the watch posts on either cliff.
