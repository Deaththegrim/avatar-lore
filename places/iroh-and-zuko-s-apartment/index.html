Iroh and Zuko's apartment

[Recommended Level: 6-24 · Danger: High]

An upper-class residence in the Upper Ring, the home of two refugees from the Lower Ring after the tea-maker's fortune changed. A traveller calling here finds an uncle at home and a nephew out.
