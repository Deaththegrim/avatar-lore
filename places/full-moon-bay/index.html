Full Moon Bay

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 9-24 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A secluded cove on the eastern of the two lakes, south of Ba Sing Se, where the hidden ferry station takes refugees across the lake to the city. Every refugee from the south comes here with a passport and the fare or without them, and the ticket clerks of the Earth Kingdom ferry decide who crosses. A traveller sees first the cliff hiding the cove, then the crowd: families, deserters, merchants, a man with cabbages, all waiting.

Areas

  • Ferry Station: The station hall where tickets are stamped and refused, the yard clerk at the counter and the ferries at the dock beyond.
  • Refugee Camp: The tents and fires of those still waiting for papers or money, where everything is for sale and nothing is safe.
  • The Dock: The lake dock where the ferries to Ba Sing Se load, guards checking passports a second time.
