Team Avatar's Upper Ring house

[Recommended Level: 6-24 · Danger: High]

A one-storey house of three bedrooms in the Upper Ring, part of a lady's manor, given to the Avatar's party with furnishings, services and a Joo Dee free of charge. A traveller invited here is watched from the street.

Areas

  • The House: The rooms and the courtyard.
