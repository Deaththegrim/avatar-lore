Wan Shi Tong's Library

[Recommended Level: 12-27 · Danger: Severe]

An enormous library devoted to all knowledge, kept by the spirit Wan Shi Tong and his fox knowledge-seekers, buried almost to its top in the central Si Wong Desert until 100 AG, when its keeper took it back to the Spirit World. A traveller who finds the one spire above the sand climbs down into the dark with a candle.

Areas

  • Planetary Calendar Room: The chamber where the sun and moon's dates are read.
  • The Spire: The one spire above the sand and the rope down.
  • The Stacks: Shelves without end and the foxes that run them.
