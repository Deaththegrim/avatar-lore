Wolf Cove

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 1-12 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The Southern Water Tribe's village on the shore of the South Pole continent: a handful of igloos and tents behind a low snow wall, with a watchtower that leans. The Southern Raiders took the last waterbender years ago, and Hakoda took the men to war in 97 AG; Kanna, Katara, Sokka, the children and the old are what is left. A traveller sees first how small it is, then how carefully it is kept.

Areas

  • Landing Beach: The ice beach where the canoes and the cutters land and the yard clerk's job is done by whoever is oldest. The sea is the only road.
  • Snow Wall Village: The igloos and tents inside the snow wall, the fire pit, the elders' tent and the children playing at war.
  • The Ice Fields: The frozen sea beyond the village, where fishing is done and strange things surface.
