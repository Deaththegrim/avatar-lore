Ba Sing Se Zoo

[Recommended Level: 6-18 · Danger: Moderate]

The city's zoo, in the Lower Ring until the spring of 100 AG and in the Agrarian Zone after, with a keeper who cannot feed his animals and animals who show it. A traveller pays a copper to see a sad rabaroo.
