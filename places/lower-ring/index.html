Lower Ring

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 6-30 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The outermost residential ring of Ba Sing Se, home to the city's poor and most of its people: industries, food, cloth, medicine, slums, and the refugees the ferry brings every day. The Dai Li police it from the shadows and the law ignores it otherwise. The Pao Family Tea House is here, and so, in the spring of 100 AG, are two Fire Nation refugees under false names. A traveller sees first the monorail terminus, the wall above everything, and the Joo Dee smile of the woman assigned to them.

Areas

  • Monorail Terminus: The station where the monorail from the Outer Wall ends and the ferries' passengers arrive, the yard clerk's window for passage out, and a Joo Dee waiting for each new face.
  • Pao Family Tea House: A small tea house in a poor street, where an old man serves the best tea in the ring and his nephew sulks.
  • The Slums: The crowded alleys where refugees sleep and crime lives, and where the Dai Li's rock gloves come at night.
  • Workshop Street: The ring's trades: smiths, dyers, potters, the smell of work and the sound of the monorail overhead.
