Foggy Swamp Tribe

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 6-18 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

The village of the Foggy Swamp Tribe, one of the three Water Tribes, descended from southerners who settled the swamp long ago and bend the water in vines and fog their own way. A traveller is fed possum chicken and told the swamp is one tree.
