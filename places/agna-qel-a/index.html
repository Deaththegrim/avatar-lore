Agna Qel'a

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-30 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The capital of the Northern Water Tribe at the top of the world, a city built almost entirely of ice on the shore of the Northern Sea, tiered and carved, with canals for streets and a great wall facing the sea. Chief Arnook rules from the Northern Water Tribe Royal Palace; Master Pakku teaches waterbending to the men and the healers teach the women. The city has never fallen. A traveller sees first the sea wall opening, then the whole city rising in ice behind it.

Areas

  • Ice Palace: The Northern Water Tribe Royal Palace at the city's top, Chief Arnook's hall and the feast given for guests.
  • Sea Gate: The opening in the sea wall where the waterbenders raise the ice to let a boat in, and the harbour steps where passage is booked with the harbour master.
  • The Canals: The ice canals that are the city's streets, boats poled along them under carved bridges.
  • Training Grounds: The ice fields where Master Pakku drills his students and refuses the women.
