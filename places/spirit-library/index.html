Spirit Library

[Recommended Level: 20-40 · Danger: Deadly]

Wan Shi Tong's Library on the Spirit World's side, where it stands whole after its keeper pulled it out of the desert in 100 AG, the knowledge of the world on its shelves and the owl who no longer trusts humans at its door. A traveller who comes as a spirit may read; one who comes with a question of war is refused.
