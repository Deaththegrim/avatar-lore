Roku's island

[Recommended Level: 6-18 · Danger: Moderate]

A small isle that was Avatar Roku's home in his later years, with a cottage where he lived with Ta Min and a cave in the cliff behind it where his dragon lived, until the eruption that killed him. A village once stood here too. A traveller finds ash, ruin and a very old silence.

Areas

  • The Ruin: What the eruption left of Roku's cottage and the village below it.
