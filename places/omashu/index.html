Omashu

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 6-24 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The second city of the Earth Kingdom, built up a peak of the Kolau Mountain Range in stepped terraces, with the delivery system's chutes running down it on earthbending and gravity. An army of earthbenders guards the gate, and King Bumi, old, mad and the strongest earthbender alive, rules it. Omashu holds free until the spring of 100 AG, when it surrenders and becomes New Ozai. A traveller sees first the bridge to the gate, the guards' rock, and the packages flying overhead.

Areas

  • Gate Yard: The yard inside the great gate where the coaches and ostrich-horse carts wait and the yard clerk books passage. Earthbender guards check every cart's cabbages.
  • Lower Terraces: The markets and houses of the lower city, the mountain wind in every street and the chutes' rumble overhead.
  • Peak Palace: King Bumi's palace at the peak, with its throne room, its feasts and its three challenges for anyone he finds interesting.
  • The Chutes: The delivery system's stone chutes running down the terraces, packages and the occasional fool sliding through the city on earthbending and gravity.
