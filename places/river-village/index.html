River village

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 3-18 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A medium-sized Earth Kingdom town between the forks of a large river on the Mo Ce coast, a regional trading hub where merchants sell whatever the river brings. Pirates trade openly from their ship at the quay, scrolls and curiosities among the rest, and nobody asks where the cargo came from. A traveller sees first the bridges, the stalls and the mixed crowd of farmers, river boatmen and men with too many knives.

Areas

  • Forks Landing: The landing where the river boats, the coast ferries and the coaches meet, the yard clerk's shed at its head and the fares chalked on a board.
  • Market Bridges: The stalls on the bridges between the forks: food, cloth, charms, maps, and the pirates' shop with its curiosities and its guard.
  • Pirate Quay: The quay where the pirates' ship ties up, a reptile bird on its rail and the captain's men watching the town watch them.
