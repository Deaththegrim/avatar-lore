Gaoling

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 3-15 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A large, old town in the mountains of the southern Earth Kingdom, rich at the top and poor at the bottom, with the Beifong Estate above it and Earth Rumble, the earthbending tournament, in its arena. Master Yu's academy teaches the rich; Xin Fu runs the tournament for everyone else. The war has not come here and the town acts as if it never will. A traveller sees first the gate, the caravan yard, and the posters for Earth Rumble.

Areas

  • Beifong Estate: The walled estate of the richest family in the town, gardens, guards and a daughter nobody outside has seen.
  • Caravan Yard: The yard by the town gate where the coaches and caravans load, the yard clerk's table under an awning and ostrich horses in the pens.
  • Earth Rumble Arena: The rock arena where Earth Rumble is fought, the Boulder and the rest throwing rocks for a crowd that pays for it.
  • Master Yu's Academy: The earthbending academy where the town's sons learn forms they will never use.
