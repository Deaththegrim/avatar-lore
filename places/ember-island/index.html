Ember Island

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 6-24 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The Fire Nation's resort island in the outer islands to the north: black-sand beaches, palm trees, luxurious vacation homes for the wealthy and the powerful, kuai ball on the sand and the Ember Island Players at the theater. High officials keep houses here, and the Fire Nation Royal Family keeps a beach house its children have not visited in years. A traveller sees first the ferry pier, the parasols and the ease of people who have never been near the war they are winning.

Areas

  • Ferry Pier: The pier where the ferries and the Fire Navy's passenger sloops tie up, with the yard clerk's booth at its landward end and porters waiting for the rich.
  • The Beach: The black-sand beach below the resort houses: kuai ball nets, parasols, the bonfires of the young and rich at night.
  • The Royal Beach House: The Fire Nation Royal Family's house on the shore, shuttered for years, its garden gone wild and its portraits of a family that no longer exists.
  • Theater Steps: The steps and foyer of the theater where the Ember Island Players perform, famous and terrible, with the island's whole society in the seats.
