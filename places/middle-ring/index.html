Middle Ring

[Recommended Level: 6-30 · Danger: Severe]

The middle ring of Ba Sing Se, the home of its merchants, scholars and craftsmen: Kuanming Square, the university, the Firelight Fountain and the shops that serve the upper ring. A traveller with money walks here freely; without it, briefly.

Areas

  • Kuanming Square: The square and its restaurants.
  • Shop Street: The shops of the ring.

People here

  • The Organ Grinder (Ba Sing Se citizen)
  • The Pet Shop Owner (Merchant)
