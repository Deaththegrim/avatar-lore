Piandao's castle

[Recommended Level: 8-24 · Danger: High]

The lavish estate of the sword master Piandao high on the cliff over Shu Jing: sixteen bedrooms, two gardens, a foundry where swords are made, stables, and a white lotus on the front gate and the courtyard floor. He takes the student he chooses and sends the rest away. A traveller is received by the butler and judged.

Areas

  • Foundry: The forge where the master's swords are made.
  • Lotus Courtyard: The lotus courtyard where the master tests a student with a brush, a stone and a sword.

People here

  • Fat (Fire Nation civilian)
  • Piandao (Fire Nation noble)
