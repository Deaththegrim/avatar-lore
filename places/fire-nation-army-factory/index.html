Fire Nation Army factory

[Recommended Level: 8-24 · Danger: High]

The army's smelting plant on the shore of the Jang Hui River, where metals for weapons and vehicles are made and a tough new armour steel is cooled with river water. Its smokestacks foul the air and its waste fouls the river that feeds Jang Hui. A traveller smells it before seeing it.

Areas

  • Smelting Floor: The furnaces and the vats, soldiers in masks and the heat.
