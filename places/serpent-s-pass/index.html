Serpent's Pass

[Recommended Level: 12-24 · Danger: High]

A narrow strip of rock between the East and West Lakes, one of the few direct paths to Ba Sing Se, named for the serpent that guards it and feared across the nation. Most refugees take the ferry instead. A traveller walks it with the water on both sides and the serpent beneath.

Areas

  • The Gap: The place where the path goes under the water.
  • The Spine: The narrow path on the rock.
