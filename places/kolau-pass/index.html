Kolau Pass

[Recommended Level: 8-20 · Danger: High]

The Fire Nation's checkpoint on the mountain road through the Kolau Mountain Range, held from the spring of 100 AG with tanks and a barricade, where every traveller's papers are read twice. A traveller without papers turns back toward the tunnel.

Areas

  • Barricade: The barricade, the tanks and the table where papers are read.
