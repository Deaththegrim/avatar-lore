Omashu Royal Palace

[Recommended Level: 8-24 · Danger: High]

The palace of the monarch of Omashu at the top of the city's peak, its every door worked by earthbending so that no one but an earthbender can enter or leave. It holds a waterfall hall, King Bumi's pet goat gorilla Flopsie and an arena for his challenges. A traveller enters at the king's pleasure.

Areas

  • Challenge Arena: The arena where the king's tests are given.
  • Throne Hall: Bumi's throne, his feasts and his three challenges.
