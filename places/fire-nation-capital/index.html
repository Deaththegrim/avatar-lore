Fire Nation Capital

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-30 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The seat of the Fire Nation, built inside the crater of a dead volcano on Capital Island. The harbour lies below the crater's lip behind the Great Gates of Azulon; the Royal Plaza and the avenues climb to the Fire Nation Royal Palace at the crater's heart, with the Capital City Prison and the High Temple on the slopes. Fire Lord Ozai rules from here from 95 AG. A traveller sees first the steps from the harbour, the soldiers on them, and the banners: this city has never been attacked and does not expect to be.

Areas

  • Harbour Steps: The long stair from the crater harbour up to the city, where every arrival is counted by the Domestic Forces. Coaches and ferries are booked at the clerk's table at the top of the steps.
  • Palace Gate: The outer gate of the Fire Nation Royal Palace, Royal Procession guards at either side and the Fire Sages' High Temple above. Nobody passes without a summons or a title.
  • Plaza Approach: The avenue up from the great square where the Fire Lord's proclamations are read and the army parades. Statues of past Fire Lords ring it; the people stand where they are told.
  • Prison Road: The road down to the Capital City Prison, where war prisoners and the Fire Lord's enemies are held. Carts go down full and come back empty.
