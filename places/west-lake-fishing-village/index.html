West Lake Fishing Village

[Recommended Level: 9-18 · Danger: Moderate]

A fishing village on the western lake's shore under the Serpent's Pass, where the catch goes to Ba Sing Se and the fishers watch the pass for the serpent and the Dai Li for the fishers. A traveller buys fish and passage on a boat that is not the ferry.

Areas

  • Fishers' Houses: The village and its headman.
  • Fishers' Shore: The boats and the nets.
