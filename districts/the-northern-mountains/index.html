The Northern Mountains

The Northern Mountains is a district of the Four Nations. The Taihua Mountains along the continent's north, from the Northern Air Temple and the mechanist's colony down to Han Tui and Chameleon Bay, where the Southern Water Tribe's men hold an inlet against the Fire Navy. The temple's gliders and machines are the war's newest things; the mountains are the war's oldest ground. The roads are high, cold and few. Its hub is Northern Air Temple; its other places: Taihua Mountains, Han Tui, Chameleon Bay, Taihua High Road, Northern Coast Watchtower, Mountain Hermitage. By land it joins Ba Sing Se, Si Wong and the Plains, The Mo Ce Coast; by sea The Northern Water Tribe (across the Northern Sea).
