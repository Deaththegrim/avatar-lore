The Mo Ce Coast

The Mo Ce Coast is a district of the Four Nations. The Earth Kingdom's western shore on the Mo Ce Sea: forest and river country with the river village at the forks, Senlin Village, Gaipan and the Freedom Fighters' trees, Taku's ruins, Makapu Village under its volcano, the Abbey, General Fong's fortress and the Pohuai Stronghold where the Yuyan Archers keep the Fire Nation colonies' northern edge. The war has been here longest: burned forest, occupied villages, a prison rig offshore. The roads are old and watched, and the coast is where pirates and smugglers sell what the armies lose. Its hub is River village; its other places: Gaipan, Taku, Pohuai Stronghold, Mining village, Seaport village, General Fong's fortress, Garsai, Senlin Village, Scorched forest, Makapu Village, Mt. Makapu, Abbey, Harbor town, Roadside kiosk, Tavern, Prison rig, Fire Nation colonial village, Village resort, Seedy merchants pier, Waterfall lagoon. By land it joins Kolau and the South-West, The Northern Mountains; by sea The Fire Islands (across the Mo Ce Sea), The Northern Water Tribe (across the Northern Sea).
