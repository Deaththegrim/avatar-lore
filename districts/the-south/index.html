The South

The South is a district of the Four Nations. The Southern Archipelago and the South Pole continent: the Southern Air Temple on its peak in the Patola Mountain Range, Whaletail Island, and the Southern Water Tribe's village at Wolf Cove, stripped of its waterbenders by the raids and of its men by the war. Hakoda's warriors left in 97 AG; Kanna, Katara and Sokka and the children and old people remain. There are no roads, only the ice, the cutters and the sea. Its hub is Wolf Cove; its other places: Southern Air Temple, Patola Mountain Range, Whaletail Island, Shipwreck, South Pole Ice, Iceberg Field, Penguin Slopes, Old Southern Harbour. By land it joins nothing; by sea The Southern Coast (across the South Sea).
