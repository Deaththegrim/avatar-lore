Ba Sing Se

Ba Sing Se is a district of the Four Nations. The capital of the Earth Kingdom and the last great stronghold of the war: the Outer Wall, the Agrarian Zone, the Inner Wall, and the Lower Ring, the Middle Ring and the Upper Ring inside it, the Earth Kingdom Royal Palace at the centre, Lake Laogai beside it and the monorail over all of it. The Dai Li keep the city, and the city keeps its peace by never mentioning the war. In the spring of 100 AG the drill comes to the Outer Wall and the coup follows; after it the Fire Nation holds the walls. The roads are the monorail and the ring gates. Its hub is Lower Ring; its other places: Outer Wall, Agrarian Zone, Inner Wall, Drill Site, Ba Sing Se Zoo, Middle Ring, Kuanming Square restaurant, Ba Sing Se University, Firelight Fountain, Upper Ring, Earth Kingdom Royal Palace, Jasmine Dragon, Team Avatar's Upper Ring house, Iroh and Zuko's apartment, Fancy Lady Day Spa, Lake Laogai, Ba Sing Se Prison. By land it joins The Lakes and the Serpent's Pass, The Northern Mountains.
