Capital Island

Capital Island is a district of the Four Nations. The largest island of the Fire Nation and the seat of its power: the Fire Nation Capital sits inside a volcanic crater behind the Great Gates of Azulon, with the Fire Nation Royal Palace, the Royal Plaza and the Capital City Prison in its heart. Fire Lord Ozai rules from here from 95 AG, and the Fire Army, the Fire Navy and the Domestic Forces answer to him. A stranger needs papers, a reason and a straight back; a foreigner needs more. The roads are the harbour steps and the plaza avenues, and the only way off the island is by sea. Its hub is Fire Nation Capital; its other places: Fire Nation Royal Palace, High Temple, Royal Plaza, Capital City Prison, Mai's house, Great Gates of Azulon, Harbour Quarter, Crater Rim Batteries, Noble Hill, Capital Drill Ground, Ash Market, Royal Palace gardens, Royal spa. By land it joins nothing; by sea The Fire Islands (across the Fire Nation's home waters).
