The Southern Coast

The Southern Coast is a district of the Four Nations. The Earth Kingdom's south coast along the Nan Shan River and the South Sea: Gaoling with Earth Rumble and the Beifong Estate Chin Village and its prison, Kyoshi Island with Suki's village and the Kyoshi Warriors, the quarry campsite, and the Eastern Air Temple as the far place to the south-east. The war has not reached these towns, which is why they pretend it is not happening. The roads are country roads; the sea road runs to the Southern Water Tribe. Its hub is Gaoling; its other places: Master Yu's Earthbending Academy, Chin Village, Chin Village prison, Kyoshi Island, Suki's village, Kyoshi Shrine, Kyoshi Landing, Quarry campsite, Nan Shan River, Eastern Air Temple, Coast Road Waystation. By land it joins Kolau and the South-West, Si Wong and the Plains; by sea The South (across the South Sea).
