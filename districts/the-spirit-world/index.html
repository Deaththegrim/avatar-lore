The Spirit World

The Spirit World is a district of the Four Nations. The plane beside the world where the spirits live: the Realm of Koh, the far side of the Spirit Oasis, the spirit side of Wan Shi Tong's Library. It is reached by meditation, a solstice, or a spirit's carrying, never by road, and no bending works there unless the body comes too. Koh steals faces, Hei Bai rages for a forest, and the Avatar's past lives wait to be asked. Its hub is Spirit Oasis; its other places: Realm of Koh, Spirit Library, Spirit Woods, Hei Bai's Grove, Fog Marsh, Spirit Roads. By land it joins nothing; by sea The Northern Water Tribe (across the Spirit Oasis's far side).
