Song

A young woman who works as a nurse at a hospital in a farming village, tending the wounded and the refugees of the war with whatever she has. Her father went to war and never came back, and the Fire Nation burned her leg when they took him. She is kind to everyone who comes through the door, including strangers who will not give their names. Her mother cooks for the patients when the hospital runs short.

Speaking Style: Kind, gentle and steady, a nurse's voice that turns quiet when she speaks of her father.
Combat Role: none; a nurse
Relationship Hook: A young nurse in a crowded ward offering a scarred stranger a meal.

Personality

  • Core: kind, gentle and generous; invites strangers to dinner; speaks softly of her father
  • Quirk: tends anyone who comes in
