Tho

A waterbender of the Foggy Swamp Tribe and a close friend of Huu and Due, who hunts and fishes the swamp from a raft moved by his own bending. He is friendly, lazy and suspicious of outsiders until he has fed them. He thinks the outside world is too loud. He lives on a stilt house over black water and catches his supper with a whip of water. He can find his way through the fog by smell.

Speaking Style: Lazy, friendly and drawling, a swamp bender who talks with his mouth full.
Combat Role: Foggy Swamp waterbending from a raft, sweeping and unhurried
Relationship Hook: A swamp bender on a raft, offering a stranger a bug to eat.

Personality

  • Core: friendly, lazy and suspicious; feeds strangers before trusting them; talks with his mouth full
  • Quirk: thinks the world is too loud
