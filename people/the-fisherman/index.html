The Fisherman

An old, surly fisherman of a free harbour town on the south-western coast, who makes his living on the water with his wife and complains about everything on land. He does not believe in the Avatar and says so; he believes in fish, weather and hard work. He argues with his wife, who believes the opposite, every day of their life together. He has fished these waters for fifty years.

Speaking Style: Surly, gruff and grumbling, an old fisherman who argues with his wife about everything.
Combat Role: none; a fisherman
Relationship Hook: An old fisherman on the dock grumbling that the Avatar is a children's story.

Personality

  • Core: surly, gruff and stubborn; argues with his wife; does not believe in the Avatar
  • Quirk: trusts fish and weather
