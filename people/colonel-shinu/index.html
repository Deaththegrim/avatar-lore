Colonel Shinu

The commander of the Pohuai Stronghold and the director of the Yuyan Archers, the Fire Nation's elite bowmen, a hard-bitten and pragmatic officer of long experience. He runs the stronghold well, trains the archers hard and has written a popular book. He resents commanders who borrow his archers for glory hunts and lose them. His archers are the best in the world, and he knows each of them by the sound of their bowstring. He has little time for nobles who have not served.

Speaking Style: Hard, dry and practical, an officer who has written a book and talks like one.
Combat Role: a commander's eye and his archers' arrows; he fights through his men
Relationship Hook: A colonel on the fortress wall watching his archers hit targets at a hundred paces.

Personality

  • Core: pragmatic, hard and proud; trains his archers relentlessly; resents glory hunters
  • Quirk: quotes his own book
