The Tax Collector

A Fire Nation soldier posted to the occupied mining village to collect its taxes, with a small crew of firebenders under him. The post gives him a measure of power and he enjoys it, raising the tax when he likes and taking what he wants from the shops. He arrests anyone who earthbends, and the village fears him more than his rank deserves. His crew of firebenders are young and bored and follow his lead.

Speaking Style: Loud, petty and pleased with himself, a small man with a little power and a lot of shouting.
Combat Role: firebending of a soldier, with a crew behind him
Relationship Hook: A soldier at the mine head reading out a new tax that is higher than the last.

Personality

  • Core: petty, greedy and proud; raises the tax on a whim; arrests benders on rumour
  • Quirk: enjoys being feared
