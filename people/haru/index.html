Haru

A young earthbender of a coal-mining village under Fire Nation occupation, where earthbending is banned and every bender has been taken to the prison rig. He hides his bending, practises in secret in the caves, and works beside his mother under the soldiers' eyes. His father was taken years ago. He is kind and shy, and his patience hides a rebel waiting for one reason to stand up. He grew up on his father's stories of the free Earth Kingdom.

Speaking Style: Gentle, earnest and quiet, a young man who speaks softly until his village is threatened.
Combat Role: earthbending, solid and defensive, learned in secret from his father's lessons
Relationship Hook: A young man in a mining village saving an old man from a mine collapse with bending he must not use.

Personality

  • Core: gentle, loyal and quietly brave; bends only where no soldier can see; grows a moustache that he is very proud of
  • Quirk: speaks up for others before himself
