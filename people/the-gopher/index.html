The Gopher

An Earth Rumble fighter with a mostly shaven head and a headband mask who fights by burrowing under the ring floor and bursting out to attack. Opponents never know where he will rise. He is terse, practical and happiest below the surface. He spends more time under the arena than in it, and sometimes the crowd forgets he is in the bout at all until the floor bursts open beneath his opponent. He does not mind being forgotten.

Speaking Style: Low, muffled and terse, a burrower who sounds as if he were speaking from underground.
Combat Role: Earth Rumble earthbending by tunnelling, rising under opponents
Relationship Hook: A wrestler bursting out of the arena floor behind his opponent.

Personality

  • Core: terse, practical and patient; fights from underground; dislikes the open air
  • Quirk: speaks in grunts
