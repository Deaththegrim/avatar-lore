War Minister Qin

The Fire Nation's war minister, overseer of the military's technology, who sits on the Fire Lord's war council with plans for tanks, drills and airships. Many of his inventions have failed, and he has kept his seat anyway because nobody else knows the machines as well. He is proud, nervous and political, and he measures every battle in the engines it will need. He keeps models of every machine on a shelf in his office and dusts them himself.

Speaking Style: Fussy, proud and nasal, an inventor-official who defends his machines before anyone attacks them.
Combat Role: none; his weapons are tanks, drills and airships
Relationship Hook: A minister at the war council unrolling a plan for a machine nobody has built yet.

Personality

  • Core: proud, nervous and political; defends his machines before anyone attacks them; counts battles in engines
  • Quirk: agrees with the Fire Lord a moment before he speaks
