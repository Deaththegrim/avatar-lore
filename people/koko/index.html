Koko

A girl of eight from Suki's village on Kyoshi Island, who follows the Kyoshi Warriors around, plays with the unagi-watching children and adores anything new that comes to the island. She is cheerful, curious and fearless, and she wants to be a warrior when she grows up. She asks visitors whether they are famous. The warriors let her carry their fans to the dojo. She has never been off the island.

Speaking Style: Bright, excited and quick, a little girl who squeals at anything new.
Combat Role: none yet; she copies the warriors with sticks
Relationship Hook: A little girl running up to newcomers to ask if they are famous.
