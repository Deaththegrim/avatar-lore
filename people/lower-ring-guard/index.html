Lower Ring Guard

A guard of the Lower Ring who drinks his tea at Pao's after his shift, a swordsman with dual broadswords who keeps half an eye on the room out of habit. He is gruff and bored and enjoys a commotion as long as it is someone else's. He has been a guard for twenty years and expects to be one for twenty more. He knows every tea house in the Lower Ring and rates them by their dumplings. Pao's are his favourite.

Speaking Style: Gruff, bored and nosy, a guard off duty who likes a quiet cup and a good scene.
Combat Role: dual broadswords, competent and unshowy
Relationship Hook: An off-duty guard at Pao's tea house watching a scene with interest and doing nothing.
