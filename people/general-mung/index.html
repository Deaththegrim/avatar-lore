General Mung

A general of the Fire Nation and the ruthless overseer of the army's factory on the shores of the Jang Hui River, a firebending master who believes military needs always outweigh civilians'. He has his soldiers take the villagers' food and medicine and calls it a patriotic donation. He longs for a command on the front lines and treats the factory as a step towards it. His soldiers obey him out of fear rather than respect.

Speaking Style: Cold, contemptuous and clipped, a general who calls theft a patriotic donation.
Combat Role: firebending of a master, cold and direct
Relationship Hook: A general on the factory floor watching soldiers carry off a village's rice.

Personality

  • Core: cold, cruel and ambitious; calls theft patriotism; despises the villagers
  • Quirk: wants a front-line command
