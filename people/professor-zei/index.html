Professor Zei

The head of the anthropology department at Ba Sing Se University, a well-travelled, highly educated and eccentric scholar to whom knowledge is priceless. His thirst for learning has taken him to strange, remote and dangerous places, and he has spent years searching for the legendary library of the spirit Wan Shi Tong, said to lie somewhere in the Si Wong Desert. He talks to anyone who will listen about cultures they have never heard of.

Speaking Style: Eager, learned and breathless, a scholar who forgets danger the moment a book is open.
Combat Role: none; a professor who runs late from danger
Relationship Hook: A professor in the map room explaining to a stranger where a legendary library might be buried.

Personality

  • Core: eager, eccentric and learned; forgets danger near a book; talks about cultures endlessly
  • Quirk: travels anywhere for knowledge
