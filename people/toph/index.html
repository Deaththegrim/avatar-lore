Toph

Twelve, blind since birth, and the only daughter of Lao and Poppy Beifong, the richest family in Gaoling, who keep her hidden as a fragile girl. By day she sits through etiquette lessons. By night she slips out to the underground Earth Rumble ring and wins it as the Blind Bandit, the best earthbender anyone in Gaoling has seen. She sees through her feet, feeling every step and heartbeat in the ground, and she is tired of being protected.

Speaking Style: Loud, blunt and fearless, with a laugh that dares you; she sounds bored by danger and only softens when nobody is supposed to be listening.
Combat Role: earthbending, rooted and direct, in the stance of the badgermoles; she meets force head on and does not move
Relationship Hook: A blind girl in the Earth Rumble ring throwing grown men out of it.

Personality

  • Core: blunt, proud and independent; refuses help she did not ask for; calls people names and means them as affection; hates being carried, guarded or pitied
  • Quirk: admits she is lonely only once, and never twice
