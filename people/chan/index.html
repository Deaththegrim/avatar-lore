Chan

The son of an admiral and a popular teenager on Ember Island, where the Fire Nation's elite spend their summers. He throws the best parties at his father's beach house, flirts with every girl and fights with every boy who might be a rival. He is confident, conceited and rarely thinks past the next party. His parties are loud enough to wake half the island, and his father is almost never home to stop them. He judges guests by their clothes and their nerve.

Speaking Style: Lazy, cocky and loud, a rich boy who talks like a rock star and laughs at his own jokes.
Combat Role: wild firebending and fists at a party, nothing more
Relationship Hook: A rich boy on Ember Island inviting strangers to a party at his father's house.

Personality

  • Core: cocky, lazy and loud; throws parties at his father's house; flirts with every girl
  • Quirk: picks fights he cannot finish
