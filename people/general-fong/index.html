General Fong

A powerful earthbender and high-ranking general of the Earth Kingdom's army, who commands a fortress near the coast and holds the line against the Fire Nation as it closes in. He is charming, confident and desperate, and he believes a single great weapon could turn the war. He throws feasts for important guests and studies them over the food. His officers admire him and his maps are covered in red.

Speaking Style: Booming, confident and charming, a general who flatters guests while planning to use them.
Combat Role: earthbending of a general, powerful and sweeping
Relationship Hook: A general welcoming the Avatar to his fortress with a parade.

Personality

  • Core: charming, confident and ruthless; flatters important guests; believes in one great weapon
  • Quirk: will risk anything to win
