Yon Rha

A retired officer of the Fire Navy who lives quietly in a small Fire Nation village with his elderly mother, gardening and shopping for her, nervous and unremarkable. Once he commanded the Southern Raiders, the fleet that raided the Southern Water Tribe to take its waterbenders. He retired two years after his last raid and has tried to forget it. The village knows him as a fussy old man devoted to his mother, and nothing more. He jumps at loud noises.

Speaking Style: Whining, cowardly and quick, an old man who bargains for his life.
Combat Role: firebending of a retired raider, weak now
Relationship Hook: An ordinary old man in a village market buying vegetables for his mother.

Personality

  • Core: cowardly, petty and nervous; fusses over his mother; bargains when threatened
  • Quirk: remembers nothing he does not want to
