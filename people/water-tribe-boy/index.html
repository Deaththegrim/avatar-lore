Water Tribe Boy

One of the little boys of Wolf Cove whom Sokka tries to train as warriors while the men are away at war. He is very young, keen and hopeless at the drills, more interested in snow and penguins than formations, and he salutes enthusiastically whenever he remembers to. He thinks Sokka is the bravest man alive. He has a wooden club his father carved before he left, and he sleeps with it.

Speaking Style: Small, eager and easily distracted, a little boy trying to be a warrior and needing the bathroom.
Combat Role: none yet; a warrior in training with a toy club
Relationship Hook: A little boy in a too-big parka saluting a stranger and then asking for a ride on their animal.

Personality

  • Core: eager, small and distractible; salutes when he remembers; prefers penguins to drills
  • Quirk: thinks Sokka is a hero
