Sneers

The scout of the Freedom Fighters, orphaned young, who watches for approaching soldiers and anything else of interest and warns the others with a bird call. He fights with axes, moves through the canopy without a sound, and has a sneer that earned him his name. He is loyal to the band and suspicious of everyone outside it. He was one of the first to join Jet, and he trusts the band more than anything he has ever owned.

Speaking Style: Sharp, quiet and sardonic, a scout who speaks in short bursts and whistles more than he talks.
Combat Role: twin axes and ambush from the trees
Relationship Hook: A scout on a branch giving a bird call that is not a bird.

Personality

  • Core: watchful, sardonic and loyal; whistles bird calls; suspicious of outsiders
  • Quirk: moves without sound
