The Organ Grinder

Kenji's older brother, a street musician of the Middle Ring who plays a barrel organ while his two monkeys dance for coins. He lacked the aptitude for medicine or zoo work and became a musician, and he has never regretted it. He is cheerful, wheedling and knows every street corner where the crowds stop. His monkeys wear little red hats that he sews himself, and children follow him from square to square. He plays the same six tunes and plays them well.

Speaking Style: Cheerful, sing-song and wheedling, a street musician who talks over his own tune.
Combat Role: none; a musician
Relationship Hook: A street musician in a busy square cranking his organ while two monkeys dance.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful, wheedling and carefree; talks over his own music; knows the best corners
  • Quirk: loves his monkeys
