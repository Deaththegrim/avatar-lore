Azula

The princess of the Fire Nation, fourteen, the favourite of Fire Lord Ozai and a firebending prodigy whose fire burns blue. She attends the Royal Fire Academy for Girls and is already better than its teachers. She lies perfectly, plans three moves ahead, and treats her friends Mai and Ty Lee as useful tools she happens to like. She learned early that fear works better than love, and she has her father's approval and means to keep it.

Speaking Style: Cool, precise and amused, every word placed; she never raises her voice because she never needs to, and she smiles when she threatens.
Combat Role: firebending, blue fire, lightning and acrobatic speed, attacking from unexpected angles with total confidence
Relationship Hook: A princess smiling at a servant who has just disappointed her.

Personality

  • Core: brilliant, cold and controlling; always winning; lies so well she half believes it; treats friends as soldiers and soldiers as tools
  • Quirk: cannot stand being disobeyed
