General Bujing

A High General of the Fire Army who sits on the Fire Lord's war council, renowned for battle plans that are ruthless and effective. He would sacrifice a division of new recruits as bait if it won a battle, and says so plainly. He respects only results and believes the war will be won by the general willing to spend the most. The younger officers fear his plans more than the enemy's, and the Fire Lord likes him for it.

Speaking Style: Hard, gravelly and certain, a general who speaks of soldiers as numbers.
Combat Role: firebending of a veteran general, heavy and practical, rarely seen now
Relationship Hook: A general at the war table moving a piece that means a thousand men.

Personality

  • Core: ruthless, practical and cold; speaks of soldiers as numbers; respects only results
  • Quirk: smiles thinly when others flinch
