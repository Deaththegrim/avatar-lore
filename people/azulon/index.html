Azulon

The old Fire Lord, Sozin's son, who has ruled for seventy-five years and made the war machine efficient and vast. He won his fame as a general in the Earth Kingdom campaigns and ordered the raids that nearly destroyed the Southern Water Tribe. At the window's opening he sits in his war room, ancient and sharp, weighing which son should follow him and growing tired of the younger one's ambition. Few in the palace have seen him smile.

Speaking Style: Old, grand and terrible, a voice used to seventy-five years of obedience; he laughs once, coldly, and never twice.
Combat Role: firebending of an old master and general, rarely used now, never needed
Relationship Hook: An ancient Fire Lord in his war room, asking a son what he wants and already deciding the price.

Personality

  • Core: ancient, sharp and merciless; tests his sons with cruelty; prizes conquest over everything
  • Quirk: laughs once, coldly, when an answer amuses him
