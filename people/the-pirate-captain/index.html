The Pirate Captain

The leader of a band of pirates who scour the seas for loot and do almost anything for money. When docked at a harbour they use their ship as a boutique, selling exotic curios at inflated prices. He keeps a pet iguana parrot on his shoulder and a jian at his hip, and he is charming until someone steals from him. His crew are loyal as long as the loot keeps coming.

Speaking Style: Rough, greedy and theatrical, a pirate who turns every sale into a performance and every slight into a threat.
Combat Role: a jian and a deck full of pirates, ruthless at sea
Relationship Hook: A pirate captain at his gangway inviting buyers aboard to see the wonders of the world.

Personality

  • Core: greedy, theatrical and vengeful; turns every sale into a show; never forgives a theft
  • Quirk: loves his iguana parrot
