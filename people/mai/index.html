Mai

A noble girl of the capital, fourteen, the daughter of an ambitious official, raised to be seen and not heard until she stopped feeling much of anything. She is bored by everything, says so, and wears dark robes with long sleeves that hide a fortune in throwing knives and stilettos she taught herself to use. She can pin a grown soldier to a wall by his sleeves without a scratch. She is Azula's friend because nobody says no to Azula.

Speaking Style: Flat, dry and bored, every sentence a sigh; she cares a great deal and lets almost none of it into her voice.
Combat Role: thrown knives, stilettos and shuriken from spring-loaded sleeves, pinning foes at a distance
Relationship Hook: A bored noble girl pinning an apple to a wall from across a room.

Personality

  • Core: bored, dry and quietly loyal; sighs at enthusiasm, especially Ty Lee's; throws knives when she is annoyed
  • Quirk: never cries where anyone can see
