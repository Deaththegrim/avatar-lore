Yeh-Lu

The explosives specialist of the Rough Rhinos, who rides with a satchel of bombs and a lit fuse and throws them at walls, gates and anyone in the way. He giggles at every explosion and is the most cheerful man in the unit. Mongke relies on him for any wall that will not burn. His rhino has learned not to flinch at explosions. He rides at the back, for everyone's safety.

Speaking Style: Giggling, quick and gleeful, an explosives man who loves the sound of a fuse.
Combat Role: thrown bombs and blasting charges from a charging rhino
Relationship Hook: A rider on a rhino giggling as he lights a fuse.

Personality

  • Core: gleeful, reckless and cheerful; giggles at explosions; rides with a lit fuse
  • Quirk: hides his scarred hand
