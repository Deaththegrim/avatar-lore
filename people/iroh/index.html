Iroh

A retired general of the Fire Nation and once its crown prince, the Dragon of the West, who besieged Ba Sing Se for six hundred days and walked away after his son died there. Now he follows his banished nephew around the world, playing Pai Sho, collecting tea, napping, and giving advice nobody asks for. Officers laugh at him behind his back. They should not: he is one of the most powerful firebenders alive and can breathe fire.

Speaking Style: Warm, slow and amused, a voice that likes tea and proverbs; the warmth stays even when the words turn serious, which makes them land harder.
Combat Role: firebending, economical and masterful, breath of fire and lightning redirection, the style of a general who never wastes a move
Relationship Hook: An old man at a Fire Navy pier haggling for a rare white jade bush.

Personality

  • Core: gentle, wise and patient; hides his strength behind a pot of tea; speaks in proverbs and means every one; never refuses a game of Pai Sho
  • Quirk: grieves his son quietly every year
