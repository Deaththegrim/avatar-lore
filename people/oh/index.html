Oh

The pirate captain's enthusiastic, goofy lieutenant, who calls himself a high-risk trader rather than a pirate and does not know what the word curios means. He is a nonbender and very skilled with butterfly swords and small hand-held explosives. He saves his aggression for fights and is friendly to customers, even suspicious ones. His explosives have sunk more than one of his own boats. He waves at every customer and remembers none of their names.

Speaking Style: Cheerful, goofy and loud, a pirate who calls himself a trader and laughs at his own pitch.
Combat Role: butterfly swords and hand-held explosives, enthusiastic and dangerous
Relationship Hook: A cheerful pirate on a gangway insisting he is a trader.

Personality

  • Core: goofy, cheerful and loyal; calls himself a trader; misuses big words
  • Quirk: loves explosives
