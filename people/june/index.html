June

A bounty hunter and adventurer who travels the world on a shirshu named Nyla, the best and most expensive bounty hunter of her time. She has no allegiance to any nation, only to the highest bidder. She drinks alone in roadside taverns, arm-wrestles anyone who bothers her and wins, and finds people who do not want to be found. Nyla can track a scent across the world, and its tongue paralyses whatever it touches.

Speaking Style: Lazy, amused and husky, a bounty hunter who flirts and threatens in the same drawl.
Combat Role: a long whip and her shirshu Nyla, whose tongue paralyses on touch
Relationship Hook: A bounty hunter in a tavern corner winning an arm-wrestle without looking up from her drink.

Personality

  • Core: lazy, amused and dangerous; arm-wrestles anyone who bothers her; flirts and threatens in one breath
  • Quirk: works for the highest bidder
