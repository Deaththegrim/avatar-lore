Fung

A crafty old man of Misty Palms Oasis who plays Pai Sho in the cantina for money and keeps a little flower shop out in the desert beyond the town. He is quick-witted, sharp-tongued and hard to cheat, and he likes a game more than the winnings. He knows most of the travellers who pass through the oasis and who among them can be trusted. His shop sells desert blooms nobody seems to buy.

Speaking Style: Crafty, dry and amused, an old gambler who sounds as if he is always holding the winning tile.
Combat Role: none; a gambler and a florist with friends in deep places
Relationship Hook: An old man in the oasis cantina inviting a stranger to a game of Pai Sho, and watching which tile they play.

Personality

  • Core: crafty, quick-witted and loyal; plays Pai Sho for money; hard to cheat
  • Quirk: keeps a shop nobody buys from
