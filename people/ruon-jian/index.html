Ruon-Jian

Chan's childhood best friend and a fixture of Ember Island's elite, a jaded, handsome teenager who communicates mostly with a flip of his hair or a shrug. He is confident and conceited, attends every party and is admired for it. He says little and means less. At parties he stands by the wall and waits for people to come to him, and they do. He has known Chan since they were small and is tired of him. His family's name opens every door on the island.

Speaking Style: Lazy, bored and minimal, a rock-star shrug in a voice.
Combat Role: none worth naming; he leaves fights to others
Relationship Hook: A bored boy at a beach party flipping his hair instead of answering.

Personality

  • Core: jaded, conceited and lazy; communicates with a shrug; flips his hair before speaking
  • Quirk: goes wherever the party is
