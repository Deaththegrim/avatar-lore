Zhang Leader

The chief of the Zhang, a tribe of refugees fleeing the Fire Nation towards Ba Sing Se, as unkempt and relaxed as her people. Her camp is messy, loud and generous, and she cannot stand prim people. She holds a centuries-old grudge against the Gan Jin over the arrest of her ancestor Wei Jin, who she says was a hero. Her people eat with their hands and sleep where they drop.

Speaking Style: Loud, laid back and scornful, a chief who shouts across a camp and laughs at rules.
Combat Role: a crescent sword, swung freely
Relationship Hook: A loud chief in a cluttered camp laughing at the tidy tents of her rivals.

Personality

  • Core: unkempt, laid back and scornful; laughs at rules; hates the Gan Jin
  • Quirk: generous to her own
