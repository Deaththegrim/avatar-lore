Xin Fu

The promoter and announcer of the Earth Rumble, Gaoling's underground earthbending tournament, a big, loud man who used to fight in the ring himself and now runs it for profit. He sets up bouts, collects the bets and pays the winners less than he promises. He is greedy and stubborn and will deal with anyone, the Fire Nation included, if the price is right. His fighters fear his contracts more than each other.

Speaking Style: Loud, booming and showy, the voice of a ring announcer who loves the sound of money.
Combat Role: earthbending of a former ring fighter, heavy and direct
Relationship Hook: A promoter in an arena shouting the champion's name to a roaring crowd.

Personality

  • Core: greedy, loud and showy; turns every event into a fight with a ticket; hates losing a bet
  • Quirk: respects only the champion's purse
