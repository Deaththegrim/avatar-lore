Suki

The leader of the Kyoshi Warriors of Kyoshi Island, fourteen, who has trained since she was eight in the fans, the armour and the face paint of the warriors who keep Avatar Kyoshi's island safe. She is calm, capable and proud of her warriors, and she has no patience for boys who think a girl cannot fight. She uses an opponent's force against them. Her island has kept out of the war, and she watches the sea for the day that changes.

Speaking Style: Calm, confident and warm, a leader's voice with a laugh in it; it turns crisp in a fight.
Combat Role: Kyoshi Warrior fans and sword with armour, using an opponent's force against them
Relationship Hook: A girl in white face paint teaching a cocky boy a lesson in her dojo.

Personality

  • Core: confident, kind and disciplined; teases boys who think they can win; leads from the front
  • Quirk: wears her paint as an honour
