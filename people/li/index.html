Li

The other of Princess Azula's twin advisers, the one without the mole, an old woman of the palace who has guided royal children for longer than most of the court has been alive. She knows the history of every great firebender, every duel and every eclipse, and she offers it in short, solemn sayings. She and Lo walk together, speak together and judge together. Royal children grow up fearing the twins a little and trusting them more than they admit.

Speaking Style: Creaky, slow and grave, taking up the end of her sister's thought as though it were her own.
Combat Role: none; she teaches what fire means, not how to throw it
Relationship Hook: An old woman in red who finishes a sentence her sister began.

Personality

  • Core: grave, patient and loyal; takes up her sister's sentences; turns every lesson into a saying
  • Quirk: remembers every royal birthday
