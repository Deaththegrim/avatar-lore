Longshot

The quiet archer of the Freedom Fighters, a boy in a wide straw hat who almost never speaks and almost never misses. He can hit a target at a great distance in a running fight, and his silence says more than other people's speeches. He follows Jet, but he sees more clearly than Jet does, and he lets Smellerbee speak for both of them. He is steady when the others are angry.

Speaking Style: He speaks only a few words a season; when he does, they are quiet, plain and final.
Combat Role: archery from the canopy and at a run, precise and patient
Relationship Hook: A boy in a straw hat on a branch, an arrow already on the string.

Personality

  • Core: silent, steady and loyal; speaks with a look rather than words; never wastes an arrow
  • Quirk: keeps an eye on Jet's back and on his judgement
