Madame Macmu-Ling

A great master of haiku and the strict, disciplined headmistress of a poetry school in the Upper Ring, who leads the Five-Seven-Five Society of young ladies from the city's best families. She holds haiku contests in which a single wrong syllable means expulsion, and her society has a guard who throws out the unworthy. She respects only perfect form. Her students fear her and adore her. She writes a haiku every morning before dawn.

Speaking Style: Precise, severe and rhythmic, a poetry mistress who speaks in measured syllables.
Combat Role: none; a poet, with a guard who does the throwing
Relationship Hook: A poetry mistress challenging a visitor to a haiku duel in front of her students.

Personality

  • Core: strict, severe and exact; speaks in measured syllables; expels for a wrong count
  • Quirk: admires perfect form
