The Gecko

An Earth Rumble contestant in a bright green mask who fights on all fours, scaling the arena's walls with his earthbending and dropping on opponents from above. He relies on speed and agility to stay out of reach. He taunts in a hiss and is very proud of his costume. Crowds cheer when he runs across the ceiling. He has never once been thrown out of the ring, because he is never on the floor long enough to be thrown. He sews his own costumes.

Speaking Style: Quick, hissing and gleeful, a wrestler who crawls and taunts in a sing-song voice.
Combat Role: Earth Rumble earthbending on walls and ceilings, quick strikes from above
Relationship Hook: A masked wrestler clinging to the arena wall and hissing at the crowd.

Personality

  • Core: quick, showy and gleeful; crawls on all fours; taunts with a hiss
  • Quirk: proud of his mask
