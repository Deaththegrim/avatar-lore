Weapons Shopkeeper

The owner of one of the biggest vintage weapon shops in the Fire Nation, in the town of Shu Jing, with the best selection of used weapons and armour anywhere: spears, giant axes, maces and swords. His most prized piece is an original blade made by the great Piandao. He says the story of how he got it is so incredible that he would have to kill anyone he told.

Speaking Style: Boastful, cheerful and conspiratorial, a shopkeeper who lowers his voice for his best stories.
Combat Role: none; he sells weapons rather than using them
Relationship Hook: A shopkeeper waving a blade and swearing it was made by the great Piandao.

Personality

  • Core: boastful, cheerful and greedy; tells tall tales about his stock; recommends the most expensive blade
  • Quirk: lowers his voice for secrets
