Palace Gate Captain

The captain of the Domestic Forces guard at the capital's palace gate, where the city road climbs to the royal palace. He checks every visitor, every cart and every servant against his lists, and he has turned away nobles who forgot their papers. He is stiff, loud and suspicious, and proud that nobody has ever reached the palace without his leave. His guards dread inspection days, and the palace cooks bring him the best of every feast to keep the carts moving.

Speaking Style: Stiff, loud and suspicious, a gate captain who asks every visitor for their business twice.
Combat Role: firebending of a Domestic Forces officer, disciplined and defensive
Relationship Hook: A gate captain stopping a cart at the palace road and reading its papers aloud.

Personality

  • Core: stiff, loud and suspicious; asks every visitor twice; keeps lists of everyone
  • Quirk: takes a cut from the merchants
