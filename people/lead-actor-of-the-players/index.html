Lead Actor of the Players

The leading man of the Ember Island Players, the island's famous theatre troupe, who plays heroes, princes and villains with the same enormous voice. He takes his craft seriously and his audience more seriously still, and he improvises when he forgets a line. The troupe's plays are loud, patriotic and wildly inaccurate. He has played Fire Lord Ozai, Avatar Kyoshi and a dragon in the same season. Critics on the island adore him and everyone else tolerates him.

Speaking Style: Grand, booming and theatrical, every line delivered to the back row whether it is a play or not.
Combat Role: stage combat, fake swords and real flashpowder
Relationship Hook: An actor on the Ember Island stage proclaiming a line about the Avatar to a cheering crowd.

Personality

  • Core: grand, vain and devoted to the stage; improvises when he forgets a line; speaks to the back row
  • Quirk: loves applause more than truth
