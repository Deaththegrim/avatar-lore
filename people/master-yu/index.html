Master Yu

The earthbending master of Gaoling's academy, who teaches the sons of rich families and is paid by the Beifong family to give their blind daughter private lessons that teach her nothing. He plans to open more schools when he has enough money. He is a competent earthbender, a poor teacher, and a coward when a fight goes against him. His students bow to him and laugh at him in the same afternoon.

Speaking Style: Smooth, wheedling and self-important, a teacher's voice that turns oily when money is mentioned.
Combat Role: earthbending of a schoolmaster, correct and unimaginative
Relationship Hook: A teacher bowing too low to a rich family in front of his academy.

Personality

  • Core: vain, money-minded and cowardly; takes credit for students' talent; plans a chain of academies
  • Quirk: flatters the rich
