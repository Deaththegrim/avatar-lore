Poi

One of identical twin brothers of Makapu Village who dress as mirror images, Poi with his braid on the left shoulder and his brother Ping with it on the right. Poi is the earthbender of the pair and shows it off at every chance. Both are competing for the same girl, and both consult Aunt Wu about it. The girl has not chosen either of them yet. Their mother cannot tell them apart.

Speaking Style: Showy, boastful and competitive, a twin who always speaks a moment before his brother.
Combat Role: earthbending of a village show-off
Relationship Hook: A young man bending a rock into the air to impress a girl, while his identical twin rolls his eyes.

Personality

  • Core: showy, boastful and competitive; shows off his earthbending; dresses as his brother's mirror
  • Quirk: consults Aunt Wu about love
