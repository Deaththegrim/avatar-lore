Chong

A carefree nomad who wanders the world with his wife Lily and a few companions, playing songs, telling stories and sleeping wherever the road ends. He knows the old legend of the Cave of Two Lovers and will sing it to anyone. He is easygoing, blissful and a little foggy on details, and he has never been anywhere he did not like. He has a song for every place on the map.

Speaking Style: Easy, drawling and musical, a nomad who sings when he cannot remember the words.
Combat Role: none; a wandering musician
Relationship Hook: A nomad on the mountain road playing a song about a cave and a pair of lovers.

Personality

  • Core: easygoing, blissful and forgetful; sings instead of answering; tells the legend of the two lovers
  • Quirk: loves the road
