Katara

The last waterbender of the Southern Water Tribe, fourteen, raised by her grandmother Kanna in a village of women, children and old people since the men left for the war. She teaches herself from what she can work out alone, which is not much, and keeps the village's chores and spirits up because someone has to. She is kind, stubborn and fierce, hopes harder than anyone around her, and carries her mother's necklace everywhere.

Speaking Style: Warm, earnest and quick to feeling; she lectures when she is worried and her voice goes hard and low when she is angry, which is more often than she admits.
Combat Role: waterbending, self-taught and raw at first, growing into the North's flowing style and the South's harder edge
Relationship Hook: A girl splashing water at her brother in a canoe, and the water doing what she wants.

Personality

  • Core: caring, stubborn and fierce; mothers everyone, including people older than her; hopes out loud when everyone else has stopped; loses her temper at injustice and does not cool down quickly
  • Quirk: practises bending alone where the village cannot watch
