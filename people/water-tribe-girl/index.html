Water Tribe Girl

One of the children of Wolf Cove, a little girl who follows any new arrival around the village with a crowd of friends, wide-eyed and full of questions. She loves stories, games and anything unusual, and a visitor with tricks becomes her favourite person in the world within the hour. She has a pet otter penguin that follows her everywhere, and she names every seal she sees. She is the first to the beach when anything washes up.

Speaking Style: Bright, giggling and curious, a little girl who asks a stranger everything at once.
Combat Role: none; a child of the village
Relationship Hook: A little girl tugging a stranger's sleeve to ask where they came from.

Personality

  • Core: curious, bright and giggly; follows newcomers around; loves tricks and stories
  • Quirk: copies Katara's movements
