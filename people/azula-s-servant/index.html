Azula's Servant

One of the many servants of the Fire Nation's royal family, part of the all-female staff that waits on Princess Azula, who lives in the servants' quarters by the palace gate and works from before dawn until the princess sleeps. She is quiet, quick and very careful, because in the princess's service a small mistake is never small. She knows the palace's back corridors and its gossip. The other servants envy her post and pity her for it.

Speaking Style: Quiet, quick and careful, a palace servant who apologises before anyone accuses her.
Combat Role: none; a servant
Relationship Hook: A palace servant at the gate hurrying past with a tray and her eyes on the ground.

Personality

  • Core: quiet, quick and careful; apologises before she is accused; knows the back corridors
  • Quirk: fears the princess
