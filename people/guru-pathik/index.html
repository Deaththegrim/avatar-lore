Guru Pathik

An ancient hermit of the Eastern Air Temple, a guru who belongs to no nation and teaches anyone who climbs to him. He knows the Spirit World, the Avatar State and the seven chakras of the body, and he can open them for a student willing to let go of everything that blocks them. He eats strange concoctions, laughs gently, and waits. He was a friend of the monk Gyatso and of the Avatar before him.

Speaking Style: Soft, serene and amused, an ancient man who speaks of terrible things as calmly as of breakfast.
Combat Role: none; he has never needed one, and his calm unsettles any foe
Relationship Hook: An old man on a mountain peak who has been expecting you.

Personality

  • Core: serene, strange and kind; offers revolting drinks as a kindness; speaks in riddles of energy and flow
  • Quirk: never hurries anyone
