The Ticket Woman

A strict worker at the Ba Sing Se monorail who stamps tickets for everyone boarding and refuses anyone without one. She does not smile, does not hurry and does not make exceptions. She has worked the terminus for years and can spot a forged ticket at a glance. Travellers learn quickly not to argue, because arguing sends them to the back of the line. She eats her lunch at the gate so as not to leave it.

Speaking Style: Flat, strict and impatient, a ticket checker who has said the same sentence ten thousand times.
Combat Role: none; a ticket stamp
Relationship Hook: A ticket checker at the monorail gate stamping tickets and refusing a refugee.

Personality

  • Core: strict, flat and impatient; never makes exceptions; spots forged tickets
