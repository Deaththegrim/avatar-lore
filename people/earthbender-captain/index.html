Earthbender Captain

A vengeful soldier of the Earth Kingdom who fought against General Iroh's army through the six-hundred-day siege of Ba Sing Se. Since the siege he has considered Iroh the Earth Kingdom's prime enemy and devoted himself to the city's defence. He is hard, bitter and very good at his job, and he still watches the gate for a fat old man in red. He has buried more comrades than he can count.

Speaking Style: Hard, bitter and certain, a veteran who has never forgiven the siege.
Combat Role: earthbending of a siege veteran, hard and relentless
Relationship Hook: A soldier at the gate looking hard at every face that passes.

Personality

  • Core: bitter, vengeful and dutiful; remembers the siege every day; distrusts anyone from the Fire Nation
  • Quirk: watches the gate
