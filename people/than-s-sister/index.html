Than's Sister

A refugee travelling with her brother Than and his pregnant wife Ying towards Ba Sing Se, practical and protective, who carries most of the family's bundles and does most of its arguing. She does not trust strangers and is the first to say so. She has left behind more than she talks about. She sleeps with a hand on the bundles at night. She is younger than Than and fiercer.

Speaking Style: Practical, protective and blunt, a sister who argues with her brother and then does what he asks.
Combat Role: none; a refugee
Relationship Hook: A refugee woman with too many bundles glaring at a stranger who comes too close.

Personality

  • Core: practical, protective and blunt; carries the family's bundles; distrusts strangers
  • Quirk: argues and then helps
