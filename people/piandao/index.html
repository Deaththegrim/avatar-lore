Piandao

The greatest swordmaster and swordsmith of the Fire Nation, who lives in a castle above Shu Jing and accepts almost no students. He is courteous, patient and exact, a calligrapher and painter as much as a duellist, and he tests would-be students by how they fight, not whether they win. He once served in the Fire Army and left it. His butler Fat brings visitors, and his forge can make a sword of anything.

Speaking Style: Calm, courteous and measured, a teacher's voice with humour under it; he sounds like a calligrapher choosing a stroke.
Combat Role: swordsmanship of the greatest master in the Fire Nation, using the ground, the light and the opponent's own mind
Relationship Hook: A master swordsman at his gate, asking a stranger why he wants to learn.

Personality

  • Core: courteous, wise and exacting; tests students in ways they do not notice; treats the sword as a brush
  • Quirk: keeps his secrets with a smile
