Mayor of Fire Fountain City

The town authority of Fire Fountain City, a busy port with a giant statue of Fire Lord Ozai that breathes fire over the square. He makes the announcements, keeps the officials happy and is easily cowed, coerced or bribed. He does not ask questions about the people who pay him, and he does not like trouble in his town. He opens every festival with a speech that is too long.

Speaking Style: Oily, nervous and agreeable, a mayor who agrees with whoever is holding the money.
Combat Role: none; he hides
Relationship Hook: A mayor on the steps below the fire-breathing statue, reading a proclamation.

Personality

  • Core: cowardly, greedy and agreeable; takes any bribe; makes long announcements
  • Quirk: hides from trouble
