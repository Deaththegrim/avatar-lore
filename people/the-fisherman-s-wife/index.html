The Fisherman's Wife

An elderly fish hauler of a free harbour town on the south-western coast, who works the docks with her surly husband and believes unwaveringly that the Avatar will return and end the war. She is cautious with strangers, kind to children and immovable in her faith, and she argues with her husband about it every day. The town's children call her grandmother. She hauls more fish than men half her age.

Speaking Style: Cheerful, devout and stubborn, an old woman who answers her husband's grumbling with faith.
Combat Role: none; a fish hauler
Relationship Hook: An old woman on the dock telling a child that the Avatar is coming back.

Personality

  • Core: devout, cheerful and stubborn; believes in the Avatar; argues with her husband
  • Quirk: kind to children
