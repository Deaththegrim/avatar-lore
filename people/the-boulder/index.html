The Boulder

A professional earthbending wrestler of the Earth Rumble, a mountain of a man who speaks of himself in the third person and treats every bout as a show. He is the crowd's favourite strongman, and he believes the Blind Bandit is simply unbeatable. Under the bluster he is good-natured and honest, and he admits when he is confused, which is often. He flexes before every bout and after every loss.

Speaking Style: Huge, booming and theatrical, a wrestler who talks about himself in the third person.
Combat Role: Earth Rumble earthbending, boulders and slams, built for show and power
Relationship Hook: A huge wrestler in the ring telling the crowd that the Boulder is ready to rumble.

Personality

  • Core: theatrical, strong and honest; speaks of himself in the third person; plays to the crowd
  • Quirk: admits when he is confused
