Cantina Keeper

The keeper of the cantina at Misty Palms Oasis, a shrinking desert town whose spring has nearly dried up, where caravans, sandbenders and travellers drink cactus juice and trade news. She pours the drinks, watches the Pai Sho tables and keeps the peace with a heavy ladle. She knows which travellers are headed into the desert and which of them will come back. Her cantina is the coolest room in the desert, and the only one with ice.

Speaking Style: Dry, unhurried and amused, a cantina keeper who has heard every traveller's story and believes none of them.
Combat Role: a heavy ladle and the cantina's regulars
Relationship Hook: A cantina keeper pouring cactus juice and warning a newcomer not to drink too much of it.

Personality

  • Core: dry, watchful and unhurried; keeps the peace with a ladle; trades news with every caravan
  • Quirk: believes no traveller's story
