Tyro

The leader of a coal-mining village on the Mo Ce coast and Haru's father, taken by the Fire Nation years ago with every other earthbender of the village and shipped to the prison rig offshore. On the rig there is no earth to bend, only metal and coal, and the prisoners have stopped believing they will leave. Tyro keeps them fed and decent and keeps his own hope quiet, because hope gets men punished. He is wise, patient and unbroken.

Speaking Style: Calm, low and worn, an old leader whose quiet voice still makes younger men stand straighter.
Combat Role: earthbending of a village elder, steady and strong, wasted for years on a metal rig
Relationship Hook: An old prisoner on a metal rig handing his food to a younger man.

Personality

  • Core: wise, patient and quietly brave; keeps the other prisoners decent; hides his hope from the guards
  • Quirk: counts the days without saying the number
