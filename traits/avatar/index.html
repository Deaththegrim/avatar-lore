Avatar

You are the Avatar, the spirit of the world in a person, reborn along the cycle of the four nations. Your birth art came first; the others wait for teachers, and the Avatar State waits for the moment you cannot stop it.

Gives: Spirit +2, Chi +1; Meditation +2, Spirit Sense +1; Chi +10, Health +45 (maximum and starting); technique Spirit Sight (You see and hear the spirits others cannot. Greatly improves the next action to notice, speak with or understand a spirit); technique Avatar's Calm (You find the centre your past lives kept. Greatly improves the next action to resist fear, rage or a spirit's influence); unlocks as you train: Avatar State (Meditation 1), Avatar's Air Lesson (Airbending 6), Avatar's Earth Lesson (Earthbending 6), Avatar's Fire Lesson (Firebending 6), Avatar's Water Lesson (Waterbending 6), Hear the Past Lives (Meditation 6), Air Sphere (Airbending 12), Bridge Between Worlds (Meditation 12), Compressed Stone (Earthbending 12), Five-Whip Fire (Firebending 12), Ocean's Pull (Waterbending 12), Blue Fire (Firebending 18), Eye of the Storm (Airbending 18), Fireball of the Sun (Firebending 18), Lavabending (Earthbending 18), Shift the Plates (Earthbending 18), Speak for the Spirits (Meditation 18), Wall of the Tsunami (Waterbending 18), Energybending (Meditation 24), Fire of the Comet (Firebending 24), Raise the Mountain (Earthbending 24), The Four in One (Airbending 24), The Ocean's Avatar (Waterbending 24), Wisdom of a Thousand Lives (Meditation 24).
Pairs well with: Air Temple Born, Southern Water Tribe Born, Capital Island Born. The homes of the cartoon's last Avatars.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
