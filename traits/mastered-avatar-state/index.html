Mastered Avatar State

You can enter the Avatar State at will once the power is there, and you choose what it does.

Gives: Spirit +1; Meditation +1, Resolve +1; unlocks as you train: Open a Chakra (Meditation 6), Enter at Will (Meditation 12), Let Go of Earthly Attachment (Meditation 18), Balance the State (Meditation 24).
Pairs well with: Born of Fire, Capital Island Born. Roku's mastery.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
