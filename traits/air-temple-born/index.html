Air Temple Born

You were raised in the ruins of an air temple by someone who hid there after the genocide, among empty halls, statues and the wind.

Gives: Spirit +1; Meditation +1, Athletics +1; unlocks as you train: Glider Staff Strike (Polearms 6, with Spear and Staff), Monk's Breath (Meditation 6), Temple Stillness (Meditation 12), Detachment (Meditation 18), Seven Chakras (Meditation 18, with Spiritual Projection), The Monks' Memory (Meditation 24).
Pairs well with: Airbender, Avatar. The monks' last hiding places.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
