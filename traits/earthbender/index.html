Earthbender

You bend earth: the rooted stance, the patient wait, then the whole hillside moves. Earthbenders come from every corner of the Earth Kingdom, and the art is as stubborn as its people.

Gives: Chi +2, Body +1; Earthbending +2, Might +1; Chi +10 (maximum and starting); technique Rock Throw (A stone lifted from the ground and thrown in one motion. Greatly improves the next earthbending action to strike at range with stone); technique Earth Wall (A wall raised from the ground between you and the blow. Greatly improves the next earthbending action to block or take cover); unlocks as you train: Feel the Ground (Earthbending 6), Stone Skin (Earthbending 12), Ride the Earth (Earthbending 18), Metalbending (Earthbending 24).
Pairs well with: Kolau Born, Lower Ring Born, Si Wong Born. The kingdom's hard country.

Primary Attribute: Chi
