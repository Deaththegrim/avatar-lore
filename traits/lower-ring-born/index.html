Lower Ring Born

You grew up in the Lower Ring of Ba Sing Se, crowded, poor and watched, where the walls keep the war out and keep you in.

Gives: Wit +1; Streetwise +1, Awareness +1; unlocks as you train: Alley Sense (Streetwise 6), Ferry Ticket Hustle (Streetwise 6, with War Orphan), Agent Under the Wall (Stealth 12, with Dai Li Trained), Tea House Talk (Streetwise 12), Agent Spotter (Streetwise 18), Under the Wall (Streetwise 24).
Pairs well with: Earthbender, Nonbender. A city's hard bottom.

Primary Attribute: Wit
