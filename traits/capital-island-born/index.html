Capital Island Born

You grew up in the crater city of the Fire Nation Capital, where the Fire Lord's banners hang on every avenue and a child learns the war's victories before their letters.

Gives: Wit +1; Intrigue +1, Lore +1; unlocks as you train: Agni Kai Courtesy (Resolve 6, with Agni Kai Veteran), Court Manners (Intrigue 6), Blue Spirit Dao (Swordsmanship 12, with Sword School), Circus Acrobat Strike (Chi Blocking 12, with Chi-Blocker), Palace Rumour (Intrigue 12), Royal Tutor's Lesson (Firebending 12, with Lightning Redirection), Sleeve Knives (Thrown Weapons 12, with Thrown Blades), Yuyan Pin Shot (Archery 12, with Yuyan Eye), Patron's Favour (Intrigue 18), Royal Lightning (Firebending 18, with Lightning Generation), The Fire Lord's Ear (Intrigue 24).
Pairs well with: Firebender, Noble's Page. A courtier's upbringing.

Primary Attribute: Wit
