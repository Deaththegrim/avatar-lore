Earth Armour

You pull the ground onto your body: stone plates, gauntlets and a helm that turn blows.

Gives: Body +1; Earthbending +1; unlocks as you train: Rock Gauntlet (Earthbending 6), Earth Rumble Showman (Earthbending 12, with Southern Coast Born), Stone Shell (Earthbending 12), Crystal Armour (Earthbending 18), Walking Mountain (Earthbending 24).
Pairs well with: Kolau Born, Northern Mountains Born. Soldiers of the mountain passes.

Primary Attribute: Body
