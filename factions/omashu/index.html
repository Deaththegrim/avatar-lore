Omashu

The monarchy and the earthbender army of Omashu, the Earth Kingdom's second city, under King Bumi, who guards its gate, runs its delivery system and supplied the Earth Kingdom's army with troops until the Fire Nation took the city in the spring of 100 AG. Its guards check every cart's cabbages; its king sets challenges for anyone interesting. A stranger is tested, housed and fed, in that order; a Fire Nation stranger is housed first. One sworn to Omashu serves its gate, its chutes or its court, and rises by the king's amusement, which is harder than it looks.
