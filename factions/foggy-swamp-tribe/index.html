Foggy Swamp Tribe

The third of the Water Tribes, descended from southerners who settled the Foggy Swamp long ago and bend the water in its vines and fog in a way neither pole would recognise, living in stilt huts in leaf skirts, eating possum chicken and believing the whole swamp is one tree. Huu guards it as a swamp monster. A stranger is fed and shown the banyan; a stranger who harms the swamp meets the monster. One sworn to the tribe learns the vines, the fog and the tree, and rises by understanding what the tree shows.
