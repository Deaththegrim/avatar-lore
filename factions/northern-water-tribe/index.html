Northern Water Tribe

The chiefdom of the North Pole, the oldest of the Water Tribes, ruled by Chief Arnook from Agna Qel'a and kept for a hundred years by its sea wall, its waterbenders and its isolation. Master Pakku teaches the men to fight and the healers teach the women; its customs are old and hard. It has never fallen and never fought, until the Siege of the North. A stranger is a guest at the chief's feast and a curiosity in the canals; a Fire Nation stranger is a prisoner. One sworn to the tribe keeps the wall, the canals or the hunt, and rises by the chief's word and the master's.
