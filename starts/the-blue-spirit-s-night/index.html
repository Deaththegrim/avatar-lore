The Blue Spirit's Night

ERA · Book One · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Pohuai Stronghold, The Four Nations. A Fire Nation fortress in the western Earth Kingdom near the ruins of Taku, three walls deep, commanded by Shinu, its colonel, and home to the Yuyan Archers, who can pin a fly to a tree at a hundred paces.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 9-18)

Admiral Zhao has the Avatar locked in the Pohuai Stronghold, and the whole fortress is waiting for the Fire Lord's orders. Tonight a masked thief with two swords is going over the walls, and so are you.
🎭 Colonel Shinu, Male, the commander of the Pohuai Stronghold and the director of the Yuyan Archers. Dark red armour with black plates and shoulder guards. A colonel on the fortress wall watching his archers hit targets at a hundred paces.
