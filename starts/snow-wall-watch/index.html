Snow Wall Watch

HOOK · Before the iceberg · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Wolf Cove, The Four Nations. The Southern Water Tribe's village on the shore of the South Pole continent: a handful of igloos and tents behind a low snow wall, with a watchtower that leans.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 1-12)

Every man of fighting age has gone to the war, and the Southern Water Tribe's last village is women, children and elders behind a wall of packed snow. Sokka has made himself its warrior, and he needs help.
🎭 Katara, Female, the last waterbender of the Southern Water Tribe. Blue tunic with white trim and dark blue leggings. A girl splashing water at her brother in a canoe, and the water doing what she wants.
🎭 Sokka, Male, Fifteen, the oldest boy left in the Southern Water Tribe village and its self-appointed. Plain blue tunic with white sleeves. A boy with a boomerang guarding a snow wall against an enemy that has not come in years.
🎭 Kanna, Female, the oldest resident of the Southern Water Tribe's village at Wolf Cove. Lavender and purple parka with thick white fur hood trim. An old woman at the snow wall watching the sea for a sail she has waited years to see.
🎭 Water Tribe Girl, Female, one of the children of Wolf Cove. A little girl tugging a stranger's sleeve to ask where they came from.
