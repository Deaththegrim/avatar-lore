The Foggy Swamp

ERA · Book Two · plantbending · guided
📍 Foggy Swamp Tribe, The Four Nations. The village of the Foggy Swamp Tribe, one of the three Water Tribes, descended from southerners who settled the swamp long ago and bend the water in vines and fog their own way.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 6-18)

The great swamp in the south of the Earth Kingdom takes travellers in and shows them visions of the dead. Its tribe bends water through vines and roots, and says the whole swamp is one tree.
🎭 Due, Male, a waterbender of the Foggy Swamp Tribe who lives on the stilt village with Tho and Huu. Vine wraps on the forearms. A smiling swamp man on his porch who waves and says nothing.
🎭 Tho, Male, a waterbender of the Foggy Swamp Tribe and a close friend of Huu and Due. Short messy brown hair. A swamp bender on a raft, offering a stranger a bug to eat.
