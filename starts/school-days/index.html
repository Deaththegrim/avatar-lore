School Days

ERA · Book Three · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Fire Nation school, The Four Nations. A centre of learning in the eastern Fire Nation where the children recite the oath, learn the official history of the war and are forbidden to dance.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 6-14)

There is a new student at the school in the eastern Fire Nation, from the colonies, they say. The headmaster does not like new students, and he does not like dancing.
🎭 Aang, Male, a boy of twelve frozen in a sphere of ice somewhere in the southern sea. Yellow and orange monk robes with a high collar. A glowing sphere in the ice floe, and a boy inside it who might still be alive.
🎭 Katara, Female, the last waterbender of the Southern Water Tribe. Blue tunic with white trim and dark blue leggings. A girl splashing water at her brother in a canoe, and the water doing what she wants.
🎭 Sokka, Male, Fifteen, the oldest boy left in the Southern Water Tribe village and its self-appointed. Plain blue tunic with white sleeves. A boy with a boomerang guarding a snow wall against an enemy that has not come in years.
🎭 Toph, Female, Twelve, blind since birth. Pale unfocused seafoam green eyes, pale cream sleeveless overshirt over a green tunic. A blind girl in the Earth Rumble ring throwing grown men out of it.
🎭 Hide, Male, a student and the star pupil of the Fire Nation school. Dark red and black school uniform with a high gold-trimmed collar. A bully cornering a smaller boy behind the school.
🎭 The Headmaster, Male, the headmaster of a Fire Nation school on an island town. Dark red and black robe with gold trim. A headmaster shouting at a student for not bowing correctly.
