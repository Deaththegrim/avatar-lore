The Retired Raider

ERA · Book Three · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Yon Rha's village, The Four Nations. A minor settlement in the hilly, rainy south of the Fire Nation, where a retired Southern Raiders commander lives with his mother and does the shopping.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 6-18)

A man who once led a raid on the Southern Water Tribe lives quietly now in a rainy village in the Fire Nation, buying vegetables for his mother. Someone has come a very long way to find him.
🎭 Yon Rha, Male, a retired officer of the Fire Navy who lives quietly in a small Fire Nation village. Faded dark red robe. An ordinary old man in a village market buying vegetables for his mother.
