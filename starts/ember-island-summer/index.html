Ember Island Summer

ERA · Book Three · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Ember Island, The Four Nations. The Fire Nation's resort island in the outer islands to the north: black-sand beaches, palm trees, luxurious vacation homes for the wealthy and the powerful, kuai ball on the sand and the Ember Island Players at the theater.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 6-24)

The Fire Nation's resort island is full of rich young people, kuai ball and bonfires, and the princess and her friends have come for the summer. Everyone is trying very hard to have fun.
🎭 Chan, Male, the son of an admiral and a popular teenager on Ember Island. A rich boy on Ember Island inviting strangers to a party at his father's house.
🎭 Ruon-Jian, Male, Chan's childhood best friend and a fixture of Ember Island's elite. Loose dark red and gold open robe. A bored boy at a beach party flipping his hair instead of answering.
