The Fortuneteller's Mountain

ERA · Book One · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Makapu Village, The Four Nations. A small farming village on the slopes of Mt.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 3-12)

Makapu Village lives by Aunt Wu's fortunes, and Aunt Wu says the volcano will not erupt this year. The clouds say otherwise, and so does the smoke from the crater.
🎭 Aunt Wu, Female, the fortuneteller of Makapu Village. Mustard yellow robe with a brown shawl. A fortuneteller at her door inviting a traveller in for a free reading.
🎭 Meng, Female, Aunt Wu's young assistant. Pink wrap dress with a purple collar band. A girl carrying snacks into a waiting room and staring at a visitor a little too long.
🎭 Poi, Male, one of identical twin brothers of Makapu Village who dress as mirror images. Olive-green robe with a cream spiral shoulder pad. A young man bending a rock into the air to impress a girl, while his identical twin rolls his eyes.
🎭 The Calm Man, Male, an old man of Makapu Village who has complete faith in Aunt Wu's predictions. Dark blue robe over a white collar. An old man standing perfectly still while a bear sniffs him, smiling.
