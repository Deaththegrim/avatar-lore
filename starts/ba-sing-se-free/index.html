Ba Sing Se Free

ERA · After the comet · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Lower Ring, The Four Nations. The outermost residential ring of Ba Sing Se, home to the city's poor and most of its people: industries, food, cloth, medicine, slums, and the refugees the ferry brings every day.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 6-30)

Ba Sing Se has its Earth King back, the Dai Li are scattered, and the walls between the rings are suddenly the only thing anyone wants to talk about. The Lower Ring is where the talk is loudest.
🎭 Animal Control Officer, Male, an officer of Ba Sing Se who catches stray animals in the Lower Ring and locks them up. Olive green and cream long tunic with a yellow sash. An officer on a slum street netting a stray and smiling about the price it will fetch.
🎭 Jin, Female, a refugee girl of sixteen living in the Lower Ring of Ba Sing Se. Simple pale green dress. A delivery girl in the Lower Ring grinning at a sullen young man and asking his name.
🎭 Tycho, Male, a mugger of the Lower Ring who always dreamed of going to the university. Ragged brown tunic. A nervous young man stepping out of an alley with a knife and an apology.
