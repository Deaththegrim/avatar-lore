Tea at Pao's

ERA · Book Two · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Lower Ring, The Four Nations. The outermost residential ring of Ba Sing Se, home to the city's poor and most of its people: industries, food, cloth, medicine, slums, and the refugees the ferry brings every day.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 6-30)

The Pao Family Tea House in the Lower Ring has a new old man making the tea, and suddenly it is the best tea in the city. His sullen nephew serves it. Neither of them is who they say.
🎭 Lower Ring Guard, Male, a guard of the Lower Ring who drinks his tea at Pao's after his shift. Short black hair, olive green uniform tunic with a dark belt. An off-duty guard at Pao's tea house watching a scene with interest and doing nothing.
🎭 Pao, Male, the owner of the Pao Family Tea House in the Lower Ring of Ba Sing Se. Black vest over a long-sleeved pale green shirt. A tea house owner bowing to a customer and apologising for the tea before they have tasted it.
