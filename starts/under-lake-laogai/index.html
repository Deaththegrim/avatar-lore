Under Lake Laogai

AVATAR · Book Two · the Avatar's own story · guided
📍 Lake Laogai, The Four Nations. A large lake in the Agrarian Zone, a pleasant place for a day out according to the Joo Dees, with a high-security prison under it where Long Feng interns anyone whose actions cross his plans and the Dai Li do their brainwashing.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 12-30)

The Avatar came to Ba Sing Se to warn the Earth King about the eclipse and found a city where nobody may mention the war. Now a friend has gone missing, and the trail leads under the lake.
🎭 Head of the Dai Li, Male, the representative and spokesman of the Dai Li. Dark green and black high-collared robe with a white crescent shape on the chest. A Dai Li commander in an underground hall, deciding which master to serve.
