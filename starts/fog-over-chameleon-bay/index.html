Fog over Chameleon Bay

ERA · Book Two · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Chameleon Bay Camp, The Four Nations. The camp of the Southern Water Tribe's warriors on Chameleon Bay, tents under the trees, cutters pulled up, Hakoda's map table and Bato's cook fire.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 9-21)

The Southern Water Tribe's warriors are camped on Chameleon Bay, two years from home, waiting for orders and for news. Hakoda has a plan for the eclipse and not enough people to carry it out.
