Lower Ring Arrivals

HOOK · Before the iceberg · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Lower Ring, The Four Nations. The outermost residential ring of Ba Sing Se, home to the city's poor and most of its people: industries, food, cloth, medicine, slums, and the refugees the ferry brings every day.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 6-30)

Inside the walls of Ba Sing Se there is no war, the officials say, and nobody asks twice. The Lower Ring is full of people who came from outside and are learning not to talk about it.
🎭 The Corncob Man, Male, a resident of Ba Sing Se who rides the monorail from place to place with a vacant. Baggy green robe with a wide brown collar. A man on the monorail with a corncob in his mouth, staring past everyone.
🎭 The Ticket Woman, Female, a strict worker at the Ba Sing Se monorail who stamps tickets for everyone boarding. Heavy-lidded sly eyes, loose dark olive-green robe with a crossed collar and wide sleeves. A ticket checker at the monorail gate stamping tickets and refusing a refugee.
