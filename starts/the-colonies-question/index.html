The Colonies Question

ERA · After the comet · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Fire Nation colonial village, The Four Nations. A small Fire Nation colonial village in the north-western Earth Kingdom, a cultural centre of the colonies famous for its Fire Days Festival of fireworks, masks and firebending shows.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 3-15)

The war is over, and the colonies are the question nobody can answer. Families who have lived here for three generations call it home; the Earth King calls it his.
🎭 Festival Guard Captain, Male, the Fire Army captain in charge of security at the Fire Days Festival in the colonial. Dark red and black soldier's uniform. A guard captain at the festival gate waving masked crowds through.
