Sandbenders of Si Wong

ERA · Book Two · sandbending · guided
📍 Sandbender Camp, The Four Nations. A camp of the Si Wong tribes in a dune hollow, tents and sand-sailers, a chief and his sons, the rules of the desert and no others.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 12-24)

The sandbenders of the Si Wong rule the desert by its own law: water is given, and theft is answered. Someone has stolen a sky bison, someone has stolen a sand-sailer, and the tribes are blaming each other.
🎭 Sha-Mo, Male, a sandbender of importance in his tribe. Layered tan desert robe and wrap. A sandbender elder in a hollow among the dunes offering water to strangers before asking their names.
🎭 Ghashiun, Male, the teenage son of the sandbender Sha-Mo. Layered tan desert wrappings and sash. A sandbender boy on a sand-sailer circling travellers and grinning behind his face wrap.
