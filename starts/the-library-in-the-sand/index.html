The Library in the Sand

ERA · Book Two · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Misty Palms Oasis, The Four Nations. A town on the south-western edge of the Si Wong Desert, grown from a seedy cantina around a spring of ice that is melting.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 9-27)

A professor of Ba Sing Se University is in the oasis cantina with a map to a library that no one has seen in a thousand years: the spirit Wan Shi Tong's, sunk somewhere under the Si Wong.
🎭 Cantina Keeper, Female, the keeper of the cantina at Misty Palms Oasis. A stained apron over desert robes. A cantina keeper pouring cactus juice and warning a newcomer not to drink too much of it.
🎭 Fung, Male, a crafty old man of Misty Palms Oasis who plays Pai Sho in the cantina for money. Plain brown robe with an olive collar. An old man in the oasis cantina inviting a stranger to a game of Pai Sho, and watching which tile they play.
