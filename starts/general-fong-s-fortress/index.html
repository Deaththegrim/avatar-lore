General Fong's Fortress

ERA · Book Two · 1-5 players · guided
📍 General Fong's fortress, The Four Nations. A secluded Earth Kingdom military base in the mountains of the west coast, one of the last strongholds of the Earth Kingdom in the region, with barracks, an infirmary, a temple and guest quarters.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 6-18)

General Fong has seen what the Avatar State can do, and he wants it for the war. He has invited the Avatar to his fortress, he has a plan to force it, and he has not asked anyone else what they think.
🎭 General Fong, Male, a powerful earthbender and high-ranking general of the Earth Kingdom's army. Green and tan general's armour with shoulder guards and a dark green cape. A general welcoming the Avatar to his fortress with a parade.
🎭 Aang, Male, a boy of twelve frozen in a sphere of ice somewhere in the southern sea. Yellow and orange monk robes with a high collar. A glowing sphere in the ice floe, and a boy inside it who might still be alive.
🎭 Appa, Male, a ten-ton sky bison with six legs. Small brown eyes. A huge white beast asleep in the ice beside the glowing sphere.
🎭 Katara, Female, the last waterbender of the Southern Water Tribe. Blue tunic with white trim and dark blue leggings. A girl splashing water at her brother in a canoe, and the water doing what she wants.
🎭 Sokka, Male, Fifteen, the oldest boy left in the Southern Water Tribe village and its self-appointed. Plain blue tunic with white sleeves. A boy with a boomerang guarding a snow wall against an enemy that has not come in years.
🎭 Toph, Female, Twelve, blind since birth. Pale unfocused seafoam green eyes, pale cream sleeveless overshirt over a green tunic. A blind girl in the Earth Rumble ring throwing grown men out of it.
