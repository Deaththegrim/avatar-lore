The Avatar After the War

AVATAR · After the comet · the Avatar's own story · guided
📍 Jasmine Dragon, The Four Nations. A traditional tea shop in the Upper Ring, a failing business until an old refugee tea-maker took it over and made it the best in the city, named for drama, poetry and a nice ring.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 6-24)

The Avatar faced the Fire Lord under the comet and won, and the war that lasted a hundred years is over. Now the Avatar has to learn what an Avatar does in peace, which no one alive remembers.
🎭 Iroh, Male, a retired general of the Fire Nation and once its crown prince. Dark red armoured robe over a gold collar. An old man at a Fire Navy pier haggling for a rare white jade bush.
🎭 Appa, Male, a ten-ton sky bison with six legs. Small brown eyes. A huge white beast asleep in the ice beside the glowing sphere.
🎭 Katara, Female, the last waterbender of the Southern Water Tribe. Blue tunic with white trim and dark blue leggings. A girl splashing water at her brother in a canoe, and the water doing what she wants.
🎭 Sokka, Male, Fifteen, the oldest boy left in the Southern Water Tribe village and its self-appointed. Plain blue tunic with white sleeves. A boy with a boomerang guarding a snow wall against an enemy that has not come in years.
🎭 Suki, Female, the leader of the Kyoshi Warriors of Kyoshi Island. Pale olive green kimono armour with a dark brown chest plate. A girl in white face paint teaching a cocky boy a lesson in her dojo.
🎭 Toph, Female, Twelve, blind since birth. Pale unfocused seafoam green eyes, pale cream sleeveless overshirt over a green tunic. A blind girl in the Earth Rumble ring throwing grown men out of it.
