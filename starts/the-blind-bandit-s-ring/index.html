The Blind Bandit's Ring

HOOK · Before the iceberg · metalbending · guided
📍 Gaoling, The Four Nations. A large, old town in the mountains of the southern Earth Kingdom, rich at the top and poor at the bottom, with the Beifong Estate above it and Earth Rumble, the earthbending tournament, in its arena.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 3-15)

Earth Rumble draws crowds to Gaoling every week, and there are rumours of a champion who has never lost. You came to fight, to bet or to find out what a bender can do with metal when nobody has told them it is impossible.
🎭 The Big Bad Hippo, Male, an enormous earthbending wrestler of the Earth Rumble who fights by sheer mass. A huge wrestler biting a rock in half to roars from the crowd.
🎭 The Boulder, Male, a professional earthbending wrestler of the Earth Rumble. A huge wrestler in the ring telling the crowd that the Boulder is ready to rumble.
🎭 Xin Fu, Male, the promoter and announcer of the Earth Rumble. Dark brown and olive sleeveless tunic. A promoter in an arena shouting the champion's name to a roaring crowd.
🎭 Fire Nation Man, Male, an Earth Rumble fighter who plays the villain under the stage name Fire Nation Man. A wrestler in a red costume shaking his fist at a booing crowd.
🎭 Headhunter, Male, an Earth Rumble competitor who wears colourful face paint and fights with wild. Spiky brown hair, white and yellow face paint like a mask with black eye markings. A face-painted wrestler roaring at the crowd before charging.
🎭 The Gecko, Male, an Earth Rumble contestant in a bright green mask who fights on all fours. Full bright green wrestling mask with dark eye patches and red lips covering his head. A masked wrestler clinging to the arena wall and hissing at the crowd.
