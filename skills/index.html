Skills

The skills of the Four Nations: what each covers and how hard things are.

airbending

The art of evasion and momentum: air moved in circles, never met head on, the bender always where the blow is not. A basic success turns a strike or clears a gap; a great success carries the bender or a foe across a courtyard.

Covers: air moved in circles: gusts, blades, the scooter, the spout, a leap carried on the wind; the art's ladders open the rest. Not: a glider's launch, which is athletics, and reading a spirit, which is spirit-sense. Easy: blow out every candle in a hall. Medium: leap a chasm on a gust. Hard: scatter a squad with one sweep. Very hard: ride an air scooter up the wall of Omashu. Extremely hard: stand in the eye of a tornado of your own making.

Primary Attribute: Chi

archery

The bow and the stillness it asks for. A basic success hits what was aimed at; a great success pins a sleeve to a wall at a hundred paces.

Covers: the bow on foot or mounted, the aimed shot and the volley; the eye the Yuyan Archers are famous for. Not: a thrown dart, which is thrown weapons, and spotting a target, which is awareness. Easy: hit a target at thirty paces. Medium: shoot a rope from a bridge. Hard: pin a running man's sleeve to a tree. Very hard: match a Yuyan archer shot for shot. Extremely hard: shoot a leaf from a falling branch in the dark.

Primary Attribute: Wit

artistry

Music, dance, theatre, calligraphy, the tea ceremony, the things each people reads a stranger by. A basic success pleases; a great success moves a hall to tears or to laughter.

Covers: music, dance, theatre, calligraphy, the tea ceremony, the arts each people reads a stranger by. Not: a lie on stage, which is deception, and cooking, which is cooking. Easy: play a tune at a camp fire. Medium: dance at a Fire Nation school party. Hard: write a letter in a fine hand. Very hard: hold a hall at the Ember Island Players. Extremely hard: pour tea at the Jasmine Dragon so that Iroh nods.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

Athletics

Covers: running, climbing, leaping, swimming, a glider's launch, a wall at a run. Not: enduring, which is stamina, and strength, which is might. Easy: climb a village wall. Medium: leap roof to roof in the Lower Ring. Hard: swim the moat of a stronghold. Very hard: launch a glider from the Northern Air Temple. Extremely hard: climb the Outer Wall of Ba Sing Se by hand.

Primary Attribute: Body

Primary Attribute: Spirit

chi-blocking

Covers: quick strikes on the pressure points that stop chi: a limb numbed, bending shut off for a while. Not: a fist fight, which is unarmed, and a hold, which is also unarmed. Easy: numb a guard's sword arm. Medium: drop a soldier with two jabs. Hard: shut off an earthbender mid-stance. Very hard: take a master firebender's art away in a dark corridor. Extremely hard: block the forehead of Combustion Man before the blast.

Primary Attribute: Body
