Fire Nation soldier

A soldier of the Fire Army in red and black lacquered armour and the skull-faced helm, a spear or a sword in hand and a squad around him. He holds what the army has taken, checks papers, and obeys. Nonbender; fights in formation with spear and shield and calls for a firebender when the line breaks.
