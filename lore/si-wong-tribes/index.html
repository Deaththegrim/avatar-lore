Si Wong tribes

The people of the Si Wong Desert, loosely organised tribes of nomads, guides, traders, raiders and scavengers who bend loose sand instead of solid earth and cross the dunes on sand-sailers, effectively independent of the Earth Kingdom that stops at the desert's edge. A stranger with water is a guest; a stranger with a sky bison is a sale. One sworn to the tribes rides the sailers, learns the sand, and rises by the desert's own law, which is water given and water owed.
