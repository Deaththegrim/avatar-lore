Law in Ba Sing Se

Ba Sing Se has more law than anywhere in the world, and most of it is never written down. The Dai Li keep the city for the Grand Secretariat, Long Feng, in the name of an Earth King nobody sees. The first rule is the one no one says aloud: the war is never spoken of inside these walls, and whoever mentions it, above all in the Upper Ring, is visited by quiet men in green robes and stone gloves. Each ring keeps its own people, and a resident of the Lower Ring needs leave to pass the gate inward. Newcomers are given a cultural ambassador who smiles, shows them the sights and reports where they went. Those who keep talking stop being seen in their street, and their neighbours learn not to ask. Bounty hunters get nothing here; the Dai Li need no help. A stranger must not speak of the war, must not ask to see the Earth King, must not cross a ring wall without papers, and must not refuse the tour.
