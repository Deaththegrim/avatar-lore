Swamp firefly

In the Foggy Swamp lives an insect small enough to swat that can light up the whole clearing. Threatened, or calling a mate, the swamp firefly throws out a ball of white light so bright that onlookers have to turn their faces away, and for a moment it looks far larger than it is. Predators are dazzled and the fly escapes. Swamp people trap them in lanterns, and one will light a hut for a night. A camper who flails at one in the dark should be ready for what the flash shows: the swamp is crowded with eyes, and most of them are already looking at him.
