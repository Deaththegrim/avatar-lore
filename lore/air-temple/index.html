Air temple

The air temples were the homes of the Air Nomads, four of them, built at the quarters of the world in places only a flyer could reach easily. The Southern Air Temple and the Northern Air Temple housed the monks, the Eastern Air Temple and the Western Air Temple the nuns. The southern temple stands on a peak in the Patola Mountain Range; the northern one on a snow-capped mountain over the Earth Kingdom's northern coast, where the bison polo championship was played; the eastern one on three mountains joined by bridges, where every young airbender chose the flying bison that would be a companion for life; the western one hangs upside down beneath a cliff edge north of the Fire Nation, its spires pointing down into the gorge, so a traveller can walk the clifftop above it and never know. Pilgrims of other nations were welcome. All four were emptied in the first days of the war. The northern temple has since been settled by Earth Kingdom refugees; the rest are left to the wind, and few dare visit them.
