War of Chin the Conqueror

Some three hundred and seventy years ago, in Kyoshi's lifetime, a warlord named Chin raised an army against his own kingdom. He was cruel and proud; one tale has him striking down an emissary with earthbending just to show what he could do. His armies swept across the continent until only Ba Sing Se, behind its walls, and the small peninsula where the Avatar lived were left outside his rule. Rather than break himself on the capital, Chin marched to the neck of that peninsula and demanded its surrender. Kyoshi warned him she would not let him take her home. When he would not back down she entered the Avatar State, split the land from the continent and sent it drifting out to sea. Chin stood on the crumbling edge and refused to step back until it fell beneath him, and his war died with him.
