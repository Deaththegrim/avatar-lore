Wulong Forest

The Wulong Forest is a wild stretch of the Earth Kingdom's western coast where hundreds of stone pillars rise straight out of the land, some higher than a temple tower, their tops crowned with trees and their feet lost in green forest and mist. It is called a forest for the pillars as much as for the trees. There are no roads worth the name: the ground between the columns is broken, steep and overgrown, and travellers lose sight of each other within a few steps. An earthbender finds the place full of stone to use; anyone else finds it a maze with no way to see out. Few people live near it, and those who do say it is a place to look at from a distance.
