Piracy in the World of Avatar

Piracy has always followed the sea lanes. The Fire Navy keeps the waters of the Fire Islands mostly clear, so pirates work the Earth Kingdom's coasts and rivers and the cold seas toward the South Pole. Most crews sell what they take through smugglers ashore; others sell straight from the deck and turn their ship into a floating market, and some ports open their harbours to pirates for a share of the profit. An iguana parrot on the shoulder is a pirate captain's mark of status, crews often run in families, and tattoos are common among them. Pirate crews have always mixed people of every nation. To a pirate, a stranger with a full purse is cargo, and a stranger with something rare to sell is a customer until he is a fool. Anyone wanted ashore can find a berth on a pirate ship, if he can pay or fight.
