Lu Ten

Lu Ten was Iroh's only son and, after his father, the next in line to the Fire Nation throne. He served in the Fire Army and went with his father to the walls of Ba Sing Se, where he was killed in the fighting around the time the Outer Wall was breached. His death ended the siege as surely as any defender did: Iroh withdrew soon after and has not commanded an army since. With no grandson of his eldest line left, the succession turned. When Fire Lord Azulon died, the court announced that his dying wish named the younger son, Ozai, and Iroh did not contest it. Lu Ten's birthday fell in spring, and his father is said to grieve him still.
