Law in the Fire Islands

Across the Fire Islands each town has its mayor or governor, appointed from the capital; the Domestic Forces keep the streets and Fire Navy patrols keep the harbours, while on Crescent Island the Fire Sages keep their own temple rules. The crimes are the capital's crimes, sedition first and desertion close behind: a deserter's face is posted in every port, and the navy pays well for it. Fire Fountain City and Jang Hui answer to their officials, Ember Island to whichever noble family owns the beach. The worst prisoners go to the Boiling Rock, a prison in a boiling lake that no one has ever escaped. A stranger must not ask what the war costs, must not bend water or earth where anyone can see, and must not sleep on a rich family's sand. The islands are close and gossip rides the ferries: a needless killing on one island is talk on the next by morning.
