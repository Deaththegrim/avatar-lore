Domestic Forces

The Fire Nation's law and home guard: the police of its cities and islands, the keepers of its prisons from the Capital City Prison to the Boiling Rock, the last line against an invasion and the trainers of the army's recruits. Its officers are mostly women, and they are the ones who read a stranger's papers, post the wanted notices and take a deserter away. A stranger is checked, filed and watched; a foreigner is checked twice. One sworn to the Domestic Forces keeps the Fire Lord's peace at home, guards what the war has taken, and rises by being the officer nobody gets past.
