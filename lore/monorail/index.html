Monorail

The monorail carries Ba Sing Se's people from ring to ring. Earthbent stone arches carry the rail over the great roads between the walls, and each car is pushed along by two earthbenders standing at its back. Inside are benches, hand bars for those who must stand, and paper windows that keep out the wind. The trains keep a fixed timetable and run late into the night, but a ride needs a ticket and the passport to go with it, and most of the Lower Ring cannot afford the fare. Ticket clerks are strict with newcomers; papers that look forged will not get past them.
