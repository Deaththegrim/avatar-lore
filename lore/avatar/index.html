Avatar

The Avatar is the one person who can bend all four elements, the bridge between the world of people and the Spirit World, whose duty is to keep the four nations in balance. When an Avatar dies, the Avatar is born again in the next nation of the cycle: fire, then air, then water, then earth, following the seasons. Each Avatar learns the elements in that same order, starting from their own. The sages of each nation search for the newborn Avatar; the Air Nomads set out thousands of toys before their children and watched for the one who chose the four that belonged to past Avatars. The Avatar is told on their sixteenth birthday. The last Avatar anyone knew was Roku, born in the Fire Nation, and the next should have been born an Air Nomad. No Avatar has been seen in a hundred years.
