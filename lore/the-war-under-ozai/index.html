The war under Ozai

Fire Lord Ozai has ruled since 95 AG, and the occupation has grown harder under him. Rebellion in the Earth Kingdom is put down without mercy; in many occupied towns earthbenders are seized and shipped to prisons at sea, where no earth lies within reach of their hands. Critics of the throne, at home or in the field, are silenced. In his war room generals plan as he pleases, and a prince who spoke against sacrificing a division of raw recruits was burned and banished for it. The Fire Nation holds most of the continent's west and north, the Earth Kingdom's armies are falling back toward Ba Sing Se, and the South has no benders left to fight. By this winter only the Northern Water Tribe, Omashu and the capital's walls stand between the Fire Lord and the end of the war, and Sozin's Comet returns next summer.
