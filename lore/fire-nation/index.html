Fire Nation

The Fire Nation is an absolute monarchy under the Fire Lord, home to most of the world's firebenders, its islands strung along the equator in the west with the Fire Nation Capital at their centre. It has the strongest economy in the world, the largest navy, and the forges and factories that build both. Its people are taught that they have desire and will and the drive to achieve what they want, and that the war is a gift: Fire Lord Sozin told them their prosperity was meant to be shared with the rest of the world, and the schools still teach it that way. Children pledge to the Fire Lord in class. Foreign things, foreign dress and foreign talk are viewed with suspicion, and a stranger who praises the Earth Kingdom aloud can expect a visit from the authorities. The nation is proud, orderly and loyal, and very few of its citizens have ever seen the war they are winning.
