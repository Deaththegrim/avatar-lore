Sand-sailer

The sandbenders of the Si Wong Desert cross the dunes on sand-sailers: light wooden craft on long runners, with a sail stretched between the hulls. A pilot bends whirling columns of sand behind the sail to make his own wind, so a sailer flies when the air is still. They carry few people and little cargo, but they are quick and nimble, and nothing else on the sand can catch them. Every sailer holds a compass that points not north but toward the great rock at the desert's heart, the one fixed mark in a sea of moving dunes. A traveller who finds a buried sailer has found a tribe's property, and the tribes do not forgive theft.
