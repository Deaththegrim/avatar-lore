How Kyoshi Island became an island

Kyoshi Island was not always an island. It was once a peninsula on the southern coast of the Earth Kingdom, a poor place of farmers and fishermen that merchant ships passed by. When Chin the Conqueror had taken almost the entire Earth Kingdom and marched his armies to its neck, the Avatar Kyoshi, who had made her home there, split the peninsula from the mainland and set it adrift as an island, so that no army could reach it on foot. Chin did not survive the day. The islanders have honoured her ever since: every village keeps a statue of Kyoshi, one has made a temple into her shrine, and the warriors who guard the island fight with fans and paint their faces in her style. Its villages trade elephant koi to restaurants all over the kingdom, but some of them have never let a stranger stay the night.
