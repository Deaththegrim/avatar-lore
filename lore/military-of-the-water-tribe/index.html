Military of the Water Tribe

The Water Tribes have no army to match the other nations; each tribe defends itself. The North keeps warriors under its chief, waterbenders and nonbenders both, armed with clubs, bone spears, scimitars and machetes edged with whale teeth, and its master waterbenders hold the ramparts of Agna Qel'a. No woman serves among them. A field of iceberg spikes off the city has torn open Fire Navy hulls for most of the war, and the North's catamarans thread it easily. The South was always defended by a village militia; after the raids took its benders, its warriors were nonbenders with clubs, boomerangs and spears, and in 97 AG they sailed away under Hakoda in wolf-headed armour to fight the Fire Navy. They fight by sabotage and ambush, and they make the most of very little.
