Air Nomad Genocide

The Air Nomads lived in four temples high in the mountains, places only a sky bison seemed able to reach, and the world believed them safe. The Fire Army reached them anyway, in the comet's year, while the Earth Kingdom and the Water Tribes were too busy defending their own shores to send help. Sozin knew the next Avatar would be born an Air Nomad, and the attack was aimed at that child. The monks and nuns fought harder than anyone expected of a peaceful people; at the Southern Air Temple soldiers' bones still lie in heaps where they fell. It changed nothing. The temples were emptied, their relics carried off to the Fire Islands or broken, and the few nomads who escaped were hunted down in the years after. A century on, the temples stand empty, flying bison are a tale for children, and many in the Earth Kingdom doubt airbenders were ever more than legend.
