Southern Water Tribe

The federation of the South Pole's small tribes, ruled by a council of elders who elect its chief, once thriving with a waterbending style of its own and now stripped of its benders by the Southern Raiders and of its men by the war: Hakoda's warriors have fought the Fire Navy from Chameleon Bay and the Black Cliffs since 97 AG, and Kanna keeps the village at Wolf Cove. A stranger is fed and asked for news of the men; a Fire Nation stranger is a prisoner if the children can manage it. One sworn to the tribe fights with Hakoda or keeps the village, and rises by the warriors' trust, which is given on the water.
