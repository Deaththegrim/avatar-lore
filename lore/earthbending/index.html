Earthbending

Earthbending is the art of the Earth Kingdom, and its first masters were the badgermoles, blind diggers who shaped tunnels under the mountains. Earth is the element of substance and its season is spring. The earthbender stands their ground, takes the blow, and waits for the moment to answer, a patience the masters call neutral jing: listening, seeming to do nothing, then striking all at once. The art is as strong in defence as in attack. Earthbenders raise walls, open roads through cliffs, hurl boulders and sink a foe to the knees in stone. Its weakness is ground it cannot touch: a bender on a ship's deck or a wooden floor is cut off from the source. The Fire Nation knows it, and builds its prisons for earthbenders out of metal and out at sea.
