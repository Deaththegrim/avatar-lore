Bosco

The Earth King keeps a bear. Bosco is his pet and, by every account from inside the walls, his best friend. He roams the grounds of the Earth Kingdom Royal Palace at the king's side, sits by the throne when the king gives audience, has grown fat on cakes and pies, and is the guest of honour at birthday feasts thrown in his name. Few outside the palace have ever seen either the king or the bear; the Upper Ring hears about both only through those who serve there. Visitors lucky enough to be received should be polite to the bear, since the king trusts the bear's judgment of people.
