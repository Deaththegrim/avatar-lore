Battlements

Fire Lord Sozin laid a road of switchbacks from the beach below the Fire Nation Capital up to the city and lined it with watchtowers and turrets, making it one of the most heavily fortified roads in the world. The turrets are small stone towers, each with at least two soldiers, firebenders and a chained harpoon crossbow among them, set on cliff shelves and triangular platforms above the harbour approach. They overlook the shallow, clear water of the bay, so anything that swims or sails in is seen. The road is the only way up from the shore, and strangers who walk it are watched from every bend.
