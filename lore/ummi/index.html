Ummi

Water Tribe storytellers remember Avatar Kuruk, the Water Avatar before Kyoshi, as a man who took life easily until he fell in love. Her name was Ummi, and for her he meant to settle down. Before they could marry, the spirit Koh, the Face Stealer, took her and her face with her, to punish the Avatar for his carelessness. Kuruk spent the rest of his life hunting the spirit. The tale is told as a warning that spirits remember slights, that a careless Avatar makes enemies in the Spirit World, and that even an Avatar can lose what he loves. Mothers in the North add the lesson Koh teaches everyone: in front of a spirit who steals faces, show no face at all.
