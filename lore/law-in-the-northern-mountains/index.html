Law in the Northern Mountains

The Taihua Mountains have no ruler, and the cold is the only law everyone obeys. In the Northern Air Temple a colony of refugees lives under the mechanist who rebuilt it, and his word is the colony's law: share the work, keep out of his workshops, and treat the old temple as his. Down the slopes the Fire Nation keeps garrisons in the passes and takes any armed stranger for a deserter or a rebel. At Chameleon Bay the warriors of the Southern Water Tribe hold their inlet under their own chief, and a stranger who comes by water is an enemy until he proves otherwise. Han Tui remembers a battle and little else. A stranger must not take shelter in the temple without the mechanist's leave, must not light a fire where the passes can see it, and must not come up on Chameleon Bay in a red boat. Wanted men come here to vanish, and the mountains usually let them.
