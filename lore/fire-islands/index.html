Fire Islands

The Fire Islands are the archipelago that makes up the Fire Nation, a great many islands from Capital Island itself down to rocks too small for any map. Many carry live volcanoes, and the climate runs from mild in the north to tropical in the south. The volcanic soil is rich: farms in the lowlands grow great surpluses of rice and fruit, and the ground holds metal enough that the islanders are famous smiths. Gas seeps from the earth in places like Fire Fountain City. The same mountains that feed the islands can end them; an eruption swallowed Roku's island and the Avatar with it, and islanders still watch their peaks for smoke. By the late war the islands burn coal and oil for their factories, and their own wealth is no longer enough for what the nation wants, which is one reason the war goes on. Most people live on the coasts, close to the sea and the fishing.
