Avatar Temple

Every nation built at least one temple to the Avatar, a shrine tied to the Avatar Spirit and to every life it has lived. They are holy ground, and some nations guard them closely: only the elder monks entered the Air Nomads' sanctuary, and only Fire Sages live in the fire temple on Crescent Island, behind a sanctuary door that takes five firebenders to open. The Avatar is exempt from every such rule. The sages of these temples keep watch on statues and painted hangings of the Avatars, because it is taught that their eyes light up when the Avatar enters the Avatar State anywhere in the world. They have not lit in a hundred years. Since the Avatar vanished, the Fire Sages have pledged themselves to the Fire Lord instead, and a stranger asking at a Fire Nation temple about the Avatar is asking a dangerous question.
