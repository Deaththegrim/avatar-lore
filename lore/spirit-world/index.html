Spirit World

The Spirit World lies alongside the world of people, a plane of spirits where every wood, river and animal of the human world has something old and watchful behind it. Crossing over is rare. The Avatar is the bridge between the two worlds and can cross in spirit, leaving the body behind; a few others have done it by long meditation, and some have been carried across by a spirit as it passed, which is how people vanish from the edges of forests. The walls between the worlds are thinnest at the solstices, summer and winter, when spirits come through more easily. A spirit that takes a body in the human world gives up its immortality to do it. The dangers are told plainly to children: a body left behind lies helpless until its spirit returns; a spirit that wanders may not find its way back; and somewhere over there lives the face-stealer, who takes the face of anyone who shows him a feeling. Places like the Spirit Oasis stand close to the other side, and those who live near them treat them with care.
