Law in Si Wong and the Plains

Nobody rules the Si Wong Desert, so nothing there is a crime and everything is a feud. The Si Wong tribes hold the sand by clan; a wrong done to one sandbender is a wrong done to his whole tribe, and they answer it themselves. The Misty Palms Oasis has a cantina and no constable, and the beetle-headed merchants buy whatever is brought to them without asking where it came from. On the plains the Gan Jin and the Zhang have kept a feud for a hundred years over a wrong neither tells the same way, and a traveller who walks with one is the other's enemy. The Rough Rhinos camp on the desert's edge and take contracts on anyone with a price. A stranger must not take a sand-sailer that is not his, must not drink a tribe's water without leave, and must not cross the sand without a guide. A killing here is never posted; the dead man's kin simply remember it.
