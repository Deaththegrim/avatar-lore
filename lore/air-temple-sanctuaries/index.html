Air temple sanctuaries

At the heart of the Southern Air Temple, and of the Northern Air Temple too, lies a sanctuary, a great sealed chamber whose door has no handle and no keyhole. It answers only to airbending: a breath of air through the right pipes turns the locks with the sound of a flute. Before the war, only the Avatar and the council of elders went inside. The sanctuary of the Southern Air Temple is a tall round hall where the statues of the Avatars, one for every life the Avatar has lived, stand in a spiral that climbs toward the roof in the shape of the Air Nomad sign; the monks meditated there together, and it was held the holiest room in the temple. The monks taught that the sanctuary kept the knowledge of the Avatar and that the Avatar would meet a guide there when he was ready. Since the war no airbender has been left to open the doors, and as far as anyone knows they have stayed shut.
