Military of the Fire Nation

The Fire Nation's armed forces are the strongest in the world: the Fire Army, second in numbers only to the Earth Kingdom's; the Fire Navy, the largest of all; and the Domestic Forces at home. The Fire Lord commands them and delegates through a few high generals and admirals, while the war minister oversees new weapons and machines. The army holds firebenders and nonbenders alike, with infantry, cavalry, artillery and tanks. Women have become rare at the front and mostly serve in the Domestic Forces, which police the homeland, guard its prisons and train recruits for every branch. Special units serve beside the line: the Yuyan Archers, the Rough Rhinos, the Southern Raiders, and the imperial firebenders who guard the royal family. A widely known proverb holds that all warfare is based on deception. Propaganda fills the ranks, and promotion goes to the bold.
