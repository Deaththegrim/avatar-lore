Cutter sailing ship

The Southern Water Tribe's war boat is the cutter: a deep, curved hull with a bow that sweeps high and is roped to a single wooden mast, one or two blue triangular sails, a yellow lantern hanging from the prow and the tribe's emblem flying at the masthead. Built for the icy South, it sails well anywhere. In 97 AG Chief Hakoda took the tribe's warriors north on eight cutters to fight beside the Earth Kingdom, and their boats now harry Fire Nation shipping in the eastern seas. Southern fathers build their own cutters and take their children ice dodging in them, steering between floating bergs.
