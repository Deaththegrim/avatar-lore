Body markings in the World of Avatar

Marks on the skin say who a person is and what they have done. An airbending master bore five blue arrows, one from the forehead down the spine and one along each limb, earned by mastering every tier of the art. Water Tribe warriors paint their faces black, grey and white before battle, and in the North the chief marks volunteers for a dangerous mission with three red lines on the forehead. In the South a youth who comes through ice dodging is marked for what they showed: a crescent moon for bravery, an arc for trust, an arc over a dot for wisdom. Kyoshi Warriors wear white paint with red and black, after Avatar Kyoshi's own face. The Yuyan Archers bear a dark red tattoo across the eyes. Tattoos are common among pirates and criminals too, and the prisoners of Chin Village say a tattoo is the quickest way to fit in behind bars.
