Duels (294 - 82 BG)

The people of Kyoshi Island tell the Avatar's meeting with Chin as a duel, and they tell it with some relish. Chin came to the neck of the peninsula in full armour at the head of his army and took a fighting stance before the Avatar. Kyoshi answered with a single blast of air that stripped him of armour and robes alike and left the conqueror standing in his underclothes in front of his own soldiers. He held his ground anyway. Only then did she split the land, and the rest of the tale belongs to the cliff. Islanders tell it as proof that their Avatar gave the conqueror every chance to walk away. In Chin Village the same duel is told as a murder, and a stranger who laughs at it there will regret it.
