Life in the Water Tribes

The Water Tribes are a people of the ice and the sea: the Northern Water Tribe in its city at the North Pole, the Southern Water Tribe in its villages at the South, and far away the Foggy Swamp Tribe in the Earth Kingdom's swamp, kin by descent and very different in their ways. Family is everything; every member of a tribe counts the others as kin to some degree, and children are named for relatives. Generosity is a point of honour, and the open hand of a Water Tribe trader is a byword in other lands. They live by hunting and fishing, keep stores on the ice through the long dark, dress in fur and hide, and eat sea prunes, seal jerky and whatever the sea gives up. Winter is their season, when waterbenders are strongest. A guest is fed before he is asked his business.
