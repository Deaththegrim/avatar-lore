Catgators of the swamp

The catgator is the Foggy Swamp's bottom-feeder, a squat, dark-green reptile seven or eight feet long, with a ridged back, a flat head, a mouth full of teeth, catfish whiskers on its snout and a little fin at the end of its tail. It looks murderous and is in fact docile toward people. Most catgators eat anything they come across except the swamp's big insects, which keeps a camp free of pests, and so the Foggy Swamp Tribe and some Earth Kingdom folk keep them as pets and give them names. Some are picky eaters. Its teeth are a medicine ingredient. Most outsiders find it revolting, but a swamp family's catgator is family.
