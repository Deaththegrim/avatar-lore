Fox antelope

Herds of fox antelope graze the open grassland of the Agrarian Zone of Ba Sing Se, between the Outer Wall and the Inner Wall. Each has the spiral horns and slender legs of an antelope on a fox's body: rust-red above, white at the belly, round the eyes and at the tip of a bushy tail. They eat prairie grass and other low plants. Farmers in the zone treat them as part of the landscape. Refugees who come in through the Outer Wall and see them leaping in the evening light often take them as the first proof that the city really does hold whole countrysides inside its stone.
