Sozin's imperial campaign

The war has an older root than the comet. Sozin and Avatar Roku were born on the same day and grew up as close as brothers, and Roku learned he was the Avatar at their shared sixteenth birthday. In 37 BG, with Roku away and Sozin newly Fire Lord, soldiers and settlers crossed to the western Earth Kingdom and planted the first Fire Nation colonies there, to share, the court said, the nation's prosperity with a poorer land. Fire Nation schools teach it as a generous beginning. What the Avatar thought of it is not taught at all; the court histories fall quiet on Roku's later years, and older courtiers speak of that time in lowered voices when they speak of it at all. The colonists stayed, and the oldest colonies on the western shore, with their Fire Nation streets and Earth Kingdom fields, date from that campaign.
