Fire Lord

The Fire Lord rules the Fire Nation from the Fire Nation Royal Palace in the capital, and in this century the throne's word is law without appeal. It was not always so. The first Fire Lords stood at the head of the Fire Sages as much as of the state, and for long ages they shared power with sages and noble houses; over centuries the throne took that power for itself, and by Sozin's reign the Fire Lord was master of everything. Sozin, Azulon and Ozai have each directed the war in person, bent over maps with their generals. The crown passes to the eldest child unless the ruler names another heir, and rival claimants have settled their quarrel by Agni Kai. A Fire Lord is expected to be a firebender, and no subject who values his freedom criticises one aloud.
