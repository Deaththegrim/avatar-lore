Fire Sages

The religious authority of the Fire Nation, the keepers of its temples from the High Temple in the capital to the Crescent Island Fire Temple, who crown and bury the Fire Lords, marry the royal family, and find the Avatar when he is born in the Fire Nation. They once served the Avatar; for a hundred years they have served the Fire Lord. A stranger is given a blessing and a lecture; a foreigner is given the lecture. One sworn to the Fire Sages tends a temple's flame, keeps its records, and rises by learning what the records say and when to keep silent.
