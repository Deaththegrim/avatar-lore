High Sage

The High Sage leads the Fire Sages, chosen by the other sages through a process of their own, and lives at the High Temple in the Fire Nation Capital, ready to serve the government when it calls. In ancient times the Fire Lords themselves led the sages, until one Fire Lord set himself apart as a secular ruler and left the temples to the sages; since then High Sages have led them. A High Sage crowned Fire Lord Ozai in 95 AG, and the office crowns each new Fire Lord. The sages once served the Avatar; since Sozin's day they have served the Fire Lord alone, and the High Sage's counsel goes where the throne wants it. The office's headgear is crowned with a golden flame. A stranger at the High Temple speaks to the lesser sages first, and to the High Sage only if spoken to.
