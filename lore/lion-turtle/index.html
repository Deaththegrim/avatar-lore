Lion turtle

Sailors on the far seas tell of an island that is not an island: a turtle so vast that forest grows on its shell, with the maned head of a lion under the waterline. The tales call it the oldest creature in the world, older than the four nations, and say that before people bent the elements they bent the energy within themselves, and that the lion turtles knew that art. No person living has learned it. Scholars in Ba Sing Se file all of this under legend, and no chart marks where one might be found. If a fisherman swears the island he anchored by was gone in the morning, the polite answer is to buy him another cup.
