Agni Kai

An Agni Kai is a Fire Nation duel of honour, centuries old. A challenger names the dispute; if the challenge is accepted, the two meet in an arena, a plaza or a parade ground, kneel with their backs turned, rise, shed their shoulder mantles and turn to fight at the sound of a gong. The duel ends when one burns the other, or stands at the other's mercy. The winner wins the feud with it, and the loser must keep whatever was sworn. The palace of the Fire Lord has its own dueling chamber. Once these duels seldom ended in death; since Sozin's day, sparing a beaten rival is called weakness. A foreigner who accepts an Agni Kai has made a promise in fire and will be held to it, win or lose.
