Eastern Sea

The Eastern Sea runs from the east coast of the Great Continent down to the north-eastern shores of the South Pole, dotted with islands, among them the archipelago of the Eastern Air Temple, and joined to the East and West Lakes by a narrow channel. The fishing and trading villages along it live on what the water gives them and have never been rich. In the war it is a front line: Fire Nation ships probe it again and again for a way up to Ba Sing Se by water, and Earth Kingdom forces and their allies try to keep them out. Warriors of the Southern Water Tribe sailed north to fight in these waters, and the Fire Navy has learned to fear their tangle mines, floating hide shells of seaweed and skunk fish that foul a warship's propeller. A captain in these waters keeps a lookout night and day.
