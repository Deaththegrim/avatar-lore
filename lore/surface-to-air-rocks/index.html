Surface-to-air rocks

The watchtowers around the Earth Kingdom Royal Palace keep stacks of carved stone frusta, broad at one end and narrow at the other, by the dozen. The Royal Earthbender Guards launch them in teams of four, two to lift and two to throw, and flip each stone so its narrow face strikes first and is harder to dodge. Lined up and fired in quick succession, they stop men on foot and anything that tries to come in from the sky. No one approaches the palace grounds without leave; the stones are the palace's answer to anyone who tries.
