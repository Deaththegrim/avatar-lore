Air Nomads

The Air Nomads lived in four temples high in the mountains, one at each point of the compass, and every one of them was an airbender. Children were raised by the monks rather than by their parents, and the people were led by the elders of each temple. They ate no meat, owned little and kept peace as a vow; they taught that all life is sacred and that revenge, like a two-headed rat viper, poisons the one who takes it. Meditation filled their days, and so did play: a monk might teach a novice aim by blowing fruit pies onto his elders' heads. Each young airbender chose a sky bison as a companion for life. Villagers everywhere welcomed travelling monks for their blessings and their news. A hundred years ago the Fire Nation fell on the temples; that story is told elsewhere, and the temples stand empty.
