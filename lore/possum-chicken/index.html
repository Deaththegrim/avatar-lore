Possum chicken

The possum chicken lives in the Foggy Swamp: a white-feathered bird with wings and a chicken's body, but a possum's head and a long bare tail, and like a possum it hangs upside down by that tail from branches. The Foggy Swamp Tribe hunts it for the pot, and it is the meat they serve to guests. Swamp people compare any strange meat to it the way other people say a thing tastes like chicken, and they say so even about animals a traveller might be fond of. Outsiders who eat it at a swamp fire generally find it tastes like home.
