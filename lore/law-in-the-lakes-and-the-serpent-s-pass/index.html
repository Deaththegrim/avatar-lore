Law in the Lakes and the Serpent's Pass

The Lakes belong to Ba Sing Se's bureaucracy, and the bureaucracy's law is paper. At Full Moon Bay the ferry station takes refugees across to the city, and nobody boards without a passport stamped by the passport attendant; a forged passport is the commonest crime at the station and the easiest to be caught with. Clerks take bribes, tickets go to those who pay, and a family without papers is sent to the Serpent's Pass on foot. On the pass itself there is no law but the serpent in the strait below. Earth Kingdom soldiers guard the ferries against fire, and a firebender found aboard is a prisoner on the spot. Bounty hunters are not welcome at the station; they wait on the far side of the pass. A stranger must not carry fire onto a ferry, must not travel on a passport that is not his, and must not take the Serpent's Pass alone.
