Organizations in the World of Avatar

Much of the world is run by bodies with names. In the Earth Kingdom the Council of Five commands the army from Ba Sing Se, the Dai Li keep the capital, Ba Sing Se University trains its scholars, the Five-Seven-Five Society keeps its poetry strict, and the Royal Earthbender Guards protect the king. The Kyoshi Warriors guard their island and the Terra Team holds the Outer Wall. In the Fire Nation the Fire Sages keep the temples, the Ember Island Players keep the stage, the Fire Nation circus tours the islands, and the military is a world of its own. The Air Nomads had their councils of elders. Other groups have no charter and plenty of members: pirates, the Freedom Fighters of the western forests, the Rough Rhinos. A stranger learns quickly which bodies he must please and which he must avoid.
