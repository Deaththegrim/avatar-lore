Blue jay

The blue jay is a crested songbird, bright blue on the head, back, wings and tail, white underneath and round the eyes, and the crest rises and falls with its mood. It has learned to live alongside people and is at home among the rooftops of Ba Sing Se. It does not need people, though: a flock still lives in the ruins of Taku, where no one has lived for a long time. A jay calling over an empty town is often the only sound in it. It is one of the few birds in the world that is not some cross of two other animals.
