Law in the Mo Ce Coast

The Mo Ce Coast has two laws, and they are at war. Where the Fire Nation colonies hold, a colonial mayor rules beside a garrison commander, and earthbending in an occupied village is a crime that ends on the prison rig offshore. Where the Earth Kingdom still holds, General Fong's officers keep a soldier's law and look hard at any able stranger who will not enlist. In the forests between, the Freedom Fighters keep their own rules in the trees, and the Fire Nation calls them bandits; at the Pohuai Stronghold the Yuyan Archers hold the colonies' northern edge. Pirates and smugglers trade openly at the seedy merchants pier, which asks no names. A stranger must not earthbend in sight of a garrison, must not ask a colonist which side he is on, and must not take the Freedom Fighters' help without asking what it will cost. Bounty hunters work this coast more than any other.
