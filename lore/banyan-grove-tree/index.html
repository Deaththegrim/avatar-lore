Banyan-grove tree

At the centre of the Foggy Swamp stands the banyan-grove tree, a single tree so old and so large that the whole swamp is made of it. Its branches dropped shoots that rooted and grew into new trunks, which dropped shoots of their own, until one tree became a forest miles wide; its roots run under every channel and island in the swamp. The swamp folk say this is the lesson of the place: everything is one living thing, and people who think themselves separate are branches of the same tree. They say a waterbender of their tribe reached enlightenment sitting beneath it. Some believe that a person who lays a hand on its roots and is quiet enough can feel the whole swamp through them, and find whoever is lost in it. The Foggy Swamp Tribe guards the tree fiercely, and anyone who comes to cut or burn it will meet the swamp before they meet the tree.
