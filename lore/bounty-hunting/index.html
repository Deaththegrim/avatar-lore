Bounty Hunting

Where the law cannot reach, money does. The Fire Nation posts wanted notices with a face, a name and a reward, and deserters, rebels and enemies of the crown fill the boards in every port and colony. Earth Kingdom towns post their own for bandits and thieves. The work is taken by hunters for hire: lone trackers like June, who rides a shirshu that can follow a scent across a continent and drop its quarry with one touch of its tongue, and bands like the Rough Rhinos, who take a contract on any notorious head for the right price. A hunter is paid for the wanted, not for questions, and rarely cares whether a notice is just. Against them a fugitive's only friends are distance, a new name and towns that ask nothing.
