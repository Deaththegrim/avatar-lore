Knowledge Seekers

The spirit Wan Shi Tong keeps fox-like servants who range the whole world gathering what people write down: scrolls, codices, maps, old letters. They are brown with pale patches, quick and silent, and they cross between the Spirit World and this one as easily as a dog crosses a yard. In the Si Wong Desert a traveller may see one trotting over a dune with a scroll in its jaws, heading for the tower that is the top of the buried library. Inside, they fetch books for patrons who cannot find them, and they expect a treat for the trouble. They are tireless, and the library grows every year they live. A stranger should not chase one or try to take what it carries; what a seeker brings home belongs to its master now.
